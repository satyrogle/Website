The body
of the record.

The same kernel that runs the journey, drawn as the figure it always resembled. Forty-eight sections for a spine. The currents either side are the two futures — the one without you, and the one you chose — and where they part is the difference. The centres that light are the sections your intervention actually reached.

Nothing on this plate is drawn by hand. Every line is read out of the seed. Change the seed and the body changes with it.
