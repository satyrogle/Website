Privacy policy

Last updated 18 August 2026. This policy describes what this website actually does, not what websites generally do.

What this site collects

Nothing. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers, and contains no forms. The simulation on the front page runs entirely in your browser. Marks you place in it exist only in your browser session and disappear when you leave the page.

Hosting

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, requested page and time of request) for security and operational reasons. We do not use such logs to identify or profile visitors.

Email

If you email contact@darklattice.co.uk, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and for no other purpose. We do not add correspondents to mailing lists and we do not share correspondence with third parties.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since the only personal data we could hold is email correspondence you sent us, a request to the same address handles all of it.

Changes

If the site ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so before that change goes live.
