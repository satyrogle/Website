# H1 — THE LOAD, reference board

**Governing sentence:** light is the visible cost of holding the structure
together.

**Status:** candidate at equal standing with H2 and H3. No winner.
**Binding:** the constitution's 30 banned patterns and the section 3 equivalence
rule, the kit's H1 kill conditions, and the kill words in `CLAUDE.md`.
**Rule:** every entry names a quality to extract. Nothing here is copied, and
nothing here names a build method.

Search terms are exact so any entry can be pulled up in seconds.

## I. THE LAW — light caused by force

### 1. Photoelastic stress fringes
`photoelasticity stress fringe pattern polarised light model`

A transparent model under load, seen through crossed polarisers. Light intensity
is a direct readout of internal stress. Fringes crowd where force concentrates.

- **TAKE** — light as a literal measurement of strain; the way brightness packs
  tightest exactly at contact and notch points; the fact that an unloaded region
  is simply dark.
- **REJECT** — the rainbow spectrum, which is banned pattern 4.4 outright; the
  laboratory bench; the transparent-plastic read; anything that looks like a
  diagram.
- **FRAME** — 1 and 5. This is the physical justification for the entire
  direction and the answer to "why is that bit bright".

### 2. Creep cavitation and interface delamination
`creep cavitation grain boundary micrograph` / `composite delamination stress whitening`

Material at the point of failing. Voids open along boundaries. Polymers whiten
where they have been strained past recovery.

- **TAKE** — separation as a fine bright line at an interface; stress whitening
  as a pale bloom in the material itself rather than a glow added on top;
  damage that reads as slow and already-happened.
- **REJECT** — the microscope frame, scale bars, false colour, and above all the
  fantasy "glowing crack" that the kit kills by name.
- **FRAME** — 4 and 5.

## II. MASS — weight that is not a building

### 3. Dry-dock keel blocks under a hull
`dry dock keel blocks ship hull underside`

Tens of thousands of tonnes resting on a sparse line of small contact blocks.
Everything that matters is happening where the mass touches.

- **TAKE** — colossal mass carried on few, small, deliberate contacts; the void
  under a heavy thing; the sense that the supports are undersized for the load.
- **REJECT** — the ship silhouette, the yard, cranes, orange sodium lighting,
  any read of a named vessel or industrial site.
- **FRAME** — 1 and 3.

### 4. Raw board-marked concrete undercrofts
`Barbican undercroft board marked concrete soffit` / `Boston City Hall underside`

Brutalist soffits and coffers: deep ordered mass overhead, real occlusion, light
arriving only at edges.

- **TAKE** — depth in the mass itself, coffer shadow doing the structural
  reading, an ordered surface that is clearly carrying something.
- **REJECT** — the building read, the architectural photograph, sky, people,
  any complete legible room. If it can be named as architecture it has failed.
- **FRAME** — 1 and 3.

### 5. Richard Serra plate works
`Richard Serra Sequence steel plate weight oxide surface`

Steel at a thickness where the material's weight is the subject.

- **TAKE** — plate mass, the oxide surface at grazing light, the way a single
  enormous element meets the ground.
- **REJECT** — the gallery, the torqued curve, the object-in-a-room read, and
  the sculpture-beside-copy composition the kit kills.
- **FRAME** — 3 and 4.

## III. GRADE — near-black that is still exposed

### 6. Large-format monochrome mineral landscape
`Sebastião Salgado Genesis monochrome mineral` (grade only)

Deep shadow that still contains information, specular highlights that reach the
top of the range, fine grain.

- **TAKE** — the tonal ladder only: rich blacks with detail, real highlights, no
  colour cast, film grain as texture rather than noise.
- **REJECT** — every subject he photographed. Serra Pelada is a mine, which is a
  kill word. Take the print, never the picture.
- **FRAME** — all.

### 7. Aerial perspective in heavy air
`long exposure valley haze aerial perspective layered depth monochrome`

Distance made visible by the air between.

- **TAKE** — luminance falling off with distance, layers separating purely by
  contrast, atmosphere as a depth cue.
- **REJECT** — mountains. The capability probe on 2026-08-18 proved the
  generator resolves this reference straight to aerial mountain photography.
  Atmosphere is taken; landform is not.
- **FRAME** — 1.

## IV. ANTI-REFERENCES — what H1 must never become

Mandatory group. A board without one has not defined its own failure.

| Anti-reference | Why it kills H1 |
|---|---|
| Cathedral or basilica interior | The kit's first kill condition. Ribbed mass plus light from above is one step from a nave. |
| Suspension bridge cable array | Kill condition. Repeated tensile lines read as a named object instantly. |
| Glowing crystal cave | Kill condition. Light *inside* translucent material inverts the whole law: here light is contact, not content. |
| Reactor core, containment vessel | Kill condition for frame 5. The failure basin is a broad territory, never a hot centre. |
| Fantasy glowing cracks, lava seams | Decorative light with no structural cause. Named in the kit. |
| Tron / neon edge lighting | Constitution 4.29. Light on edges for style rather than force. |
| Architectural visualisation render | Clean, evenly lit, complete, and brand-swappable — constitution section 15's first automatic failure. |

## V. WHAT THIS BOARD DECIDES

For the two frames being built now:

1. **Brightness must have a cause that is visible in the same frame.** A bright
   region without a contact, an interface or a convergence behind it is
   decoration and fails.
2. **Dark is not empty; dark is unloaded.** Peaceful mass is mass that has
   stopped carrying. That is the seed of frame 5's meaning and it must already
   be readable in frame 1.
3. **No complete silhouette.** Roof, floor and outer edge are all absent. The
   frame shows one local portion of something larger.
4. **The reveal is broader and brighter, never hotter.** The failure basin is a
   territory where load paths converge, not a source.
5. **Contacts are small; masses are enormous.** The ratio is the whole feeling.
