# Philosophy and Visual Law

## Studio thesis

Dark Lattice builds systemic products and games where rules create state and state creates consequence.

The website should demonstrate that way of thinking.

## Desired experiential arc

A useful target progression is:

```text
beauty / divinity
      ↓
curiosity
      ↓
order
      ↓
causality
      ↓
unease
      ↓
comprehension
      ↓
reinterpretation
      ↓
dread
```

This is not a demand for literal religious/horror imagery.

## Core law

The same system should become more disturbing as the visitor understands it better.

Do not solve "menacing" by adding a monster, red warning UI, occult decoration, random glitch or evil-AI copy.

## Composition

Prefer:
- monumental negative space,
- a small number of strong masses,
- severe/brutalist structural weight when appropriate,
- controlled symmetry/asymmetry,
- real depth,
- clear silhouette,
- restrained supporting content.

Avoid:
- equal-weight card grids,
- dashboard framing,
- ornamental sci-fi structures,
- generic Awwwards/Apple imitation,
- excessive decorative layers,
- effects without semantic purpose.

## Organism ↔ architecture ambiguity

When used, ambiguity should come from shared structural reading.

A support may later read as anatomy.

A recess may later read as a face/head.

A seam may reveal soft material.

Do not cover the world in biomechanical tubing or creature decoration.

## Color and material

Colour law is set by `CLAUDE.md` and it currently reads: black, bone, and at most
one event colour until a journey is approved.

Do not re-lock cyan, amber or violet out of habit. Each belonged to a retired
direction — violet and cyan to the entity grammar, amber to THE INTAKE — and
none of them is current. Do not start from the existing site's palette either;
that site is being replaced.

Do not make purple atmosphere the identity.

Use glow, bloom, blur, fog and noise only when they improve material, depth, state or comprehension.

## Typography

Typography is part of the architecture.

- large enough to read,
- controlled line length,
- clear hierarchy,
- no tiny techno captions,
- no gradient-text default,
- no enormous type merely to imitate current landing-page trends.

## Motion

Motion must have a cause:
- camera movement,
- scroll phase,
- state propagation,
- pointer proximity,
- system memory,
- focus/interaction.

Prefer:
- damped response,
- bounded amplitude,
- stable resting states,
- low visual noise.

Avoid decorative perpetual motion.

Reduced-motion users must retain the conceptual story.
