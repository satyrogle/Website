01 · The system you are looking at

14,000 cells. One monument. One law.

Nothing behind this page is a video. Every cell in the monument is a record in one seeded structure. From the water it reads as marble. Keep scrolling and it will not stay whole: the surface fails in waves, and what has been holding the shape becomes visible.

Press anywhere and the monument will take a mark from you. A new cell is seated into the face: retained, written to the record, and subject to everything that follows.

Your browser could not provide WebGL2, so you are reading the still version of this page: a true capture of the same seeded world. Everything the site says remains true without the motion.

02 · Desk42

Single player · Unity · Windows PC · Steam first

Desk42

You work a desk inside a bureaucracy, deciding people’s claims.

Every decision is written into a persistent institutional record. The record does not reset between days, it is never edited to be kind, and what the institution remembers about a person becomes what the institution does to them. You are not fighting the machine. You are the part of it that signs.

In development. The current build is a bounded causal slice, not a near-complete product.

03 · The rule you could not see

The monument looks immaculate because everything that failed is already gone.

From the water it reads as grace: one white mass, held in perfect stillness. There is no grace. There is one law, and it has been running the whole time you have been here.

A cell that fails its quota is struck from the face. Struck cells fall, and the fallen pile at the foot. The system does not repair and it does not grieve. The record lists every strike with the tick at which it happened, including anything of yours that stops earning its place.

04 · Brawler (working title)

Action roguelite · Unreal Engine

Brawler

An action roguelite where the ground itself is alive.

One spatial simulation is authoritative for movement, hazards and combat consequence. When the arena burns, floods or gives way, the change is state. It propagates, it persists through the fight, and it does not care whose turn it is. You do not play on the level. You play inside it.

05 · How we build

Rules first. Tools that prove the rules behave. Games on top.

Where determinism matters, we make it real: seeded randomness, fixed timesteps, and state that can be replayed from a seed and an input trace in the environment we test on. Simulation state is authoritative and renderers only observe it. If we cannot replay it, we do not claim it.

SatyBT, a founder-built behaviour tree framework, is public under MIT. No benchmark or simulation-scale figure appears on this page, because none has been measured.
