# THE HERO — a planet breaking apart

**Logged from Jacob, 2026-08-12.** This supersedes every previous carrier note.
Read it before touching the hero.

## What it is

A dying planet, breaking apart. Cracks opening across it, earthquakes, lava
spewing out of the fractures. It is the size of a world and it is coming apart
while you look at it.

The reference for the *surface* is the Death Star: engineered armour plating,
greebling, machined trenches. The reference for the *event* is a world
fracturing. Both at once — a made thing the size of a planet, failing.

## What is already right, and must not be redecorated

- **The texture.** The rectilinear armour plating at two scales, with grooves
  cut along the seams. Approved as-is.
- **The glow.** Light accumulated along the ray, gathering in seams and
  cavities, plus the hover response. Approved as-is, twice.

Everything else was rejected. Do not re-propose an amorphous mass, an
irregular warped body, or any shape a viewer cannot name.

## Scroll is travel across the planet

Every scroll beat lands on **a specific part of the planet you are actually
looking at**. Not an abstract camera drift — the visitor moves from one broken
region to the next, and each region is a real place on the body.

## The ending

At the floor, pull back and see the whole dying planet at once, with all its
broken parts — arranged **like a huge funnel**.

## How this carries THE CORRECTION

Unchanged, and it fits better than anything before it. The approved state is
the intact body under its own armour. Deviation is the fracture: a region
splitting, lava breaking through where the crust has failed. Correction is the
system closing the fracture and forcing the crust back to the recorded
surface. Residue is one thin violet seam where the break was.

Colour grammar is untouched: amber is the record and the light in the cracks,
cyan is deviation, violet is consequence. Lava is amber because the light in a
fracture is a record of a state, not the state.

## Guardrails that still bind

- No rings. Trenches are offset and broken, never one continuous equator.
- No visible primitives *as material* — but the silhouette must be legible.
  A body you cannot name is not the goal; that mistake cost two passes.
- Locked stack: Vite + TypeScript + Three/WebGL2/GLSL + GSAP. No new
  dependencies.
