# Decision frame, constitution 12.1

Written 2026-08-18, before the high-end pass on `claude/genesis`. Jacob saw
the running v1 on his GPU, called it a low-level tech demo, and ordered it
brought to a high-end engineered standard under the constitution.

## 1. Purpose

The Dark Lattice homepage. The studio thesis embodied: one live deterministic
world whose beauty is produced by a merciless rule, understood by travelling
through it. The page is the act of comprehension, not a brochure beside a
canvas.

## 2. The visitor's primary task

Understand what the company builds within the first screen. Reach Desk42,
Brawler and contact without decoding anything. Experience one meaningful,
recorded interaction within twenty seconds.

## 3. What must be demonstrated, not stated

- A live seeded fixed-step simulation is the page background, with the seed,
  tick and agent count stated and true.
- A press travels input, authoritative state, visible consequence, record.
- The starvation law is real behaviour: removals happen from density read
  back from the world, and each is written to the record with its tick.
- Determinism claims scoped exactly: seeded, fixed-step, replayable in the
  tested environment.

## 4. The visual mechanism

One transport world observed at three scales. Macro: an immense converging
field of held luminosity, lit as material (height-field lighting, one cool
key light), rich darks, real highlights. Descent: the same data regraded
colder and harder as severity rises. Micro: the ribbons resolve into
individual agents. Return: the opening frame, regraded, understood. The
record rendered as an institutional ledger, because the register of Desk42
is the register of the studio.

## 5. Banned patterns most likely to appear here, and the guards

- 4.1 decorative gradients: the hero scrim exists for readability only,
  single hue to transparent, never a brand device.
- 4.8 glassmorphism: content panels are solid carbon, hard edges, no blur.
- 7.2 semantic leakage: in the DOM, cyan appears only on live event data
  (placed marks) and red only on removals. Focus rings, nav state and
  selection are bone. No cyan chrome. Inside the render, the descent
  regrades the field toward cyan as severity rises: an authored
  observational regrade tied to scroll, an intentional documented
  exception, never a claim that a live deviation is occurring.
- 6 typography: no startup-default faces, no monospace-everywhere, no tiny
  labels as futurism. Two documented families (below).
- 4.14 fake terminal: the record shows only true data from the running world.
- 5.5 scroll length: every stop delivers information plus a real scale or
  severity change.
- 4.21 loading: deterministic warm-up is surfaced honestly, then the world
  fades in once formed. Hero copy renders before JavaScript.
- 4.26/4.27: Terms and Privacy pages ship with this release and describe
  actual behaviour (no cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests).

## 6. Type system, constitution section 6

- Display and reading: Archivo (variable; width and weight axes). One family
  carries a severe, slightly condensed uppercase display voice and a plain
  reading voice. Chosen for institutional documentary weight; not Inter,
  Geist or Space Grotesk, and used with an authored scale, not defaults.
- Technical and record: Fragment Mono. Courier-proportioned modernist mono:
  the typewritten institutional register that Desk42 is about. Used only for
  data, labels and the ledger, never for body copy.
- Both self-hosted from node_modules assets, preloaded, no third-party
  requests at runtime.

## 7. Evidence required for acceptance

- Real-GPU captures of every stop, desktop and mobile widths.
- A static-frame check with glow and atmosphere disabled (?flat=1) proving
  the composition works as value and silhouette alone.
- The ledger showing real placed and removed entries with ticks.
- Quality harness output: replay determinism and first-action tests passing.
- Section 13 audit and scorecard recorded before presenting.
