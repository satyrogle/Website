# THE CONGREGATION — REFERENCE BOARD

**Status:** Stage 1 of the chosen journey (docs/VISUAL_JOURNEYS.md, Journey A).
For Jacob's approval before frame boards. No implementation discussion.
**Rule:** references calibrate form, light and severity. Nothing is copied. The
constitution and the vocabulary ban (no candle, cathedral, votive imagery)
bind this board: every entry was screened against them.
**Search terms are given exactly** so any entry can be pulled up in seconds.

The board is organised by what each reference teaches: the field, the single
source, the tethers, the armature, the removal, the spent sediment, and the
grade. Then the anti-board: what this direction must never resemble.

---

## I. THE FIELD (Frames 1-2: the stratified held luminosity)

**1. Squid-fishing fleet lights seen from the ISS.**
Search: `squid fishing boats lights from space ISS Sea of Japan`.
Hundreds of human-made point lights in loose bands on pure black, brighter than
the cities on the same frame. The single best proof that a field of placed
lights can read as celestial.
Take: density, uneven banding, the void around the bands, lights as points not
glows. Avoid: the green tint of the real lamps.

**2. Globular cluster photography.**
Search: `Hubble globular cluster M13 Omega Centauri`.
Tens of thousands of near-identical points with a density falloff no artist
would compose. The natural-law feel the opening needs.
Take: number, subtle brightness hierarchy, how density carries structure.
Avoid: star colour variety, diffraction spikes, any nebulosity.

**3. Snowfall held in still night air, long exposure.**
Search: `night snowfall long exposure floodlight still air`.
Suspended pale points with real atmospheric depth between camera and source.
Take: haze as depth cue, points dimming with distance, absolute stillness.
Avoid: warm sodium colour, ground, any visible lamp.

**4. Siphonophore colonies, deep-sea ROV footage.**
Search: `MBARI siphonophore Marrus orthocanna`.
A colonial animal: hundreds of IDENTICAL luminous units in an organic
arrangement on black water. The only biological reference on the board, here
because identical-units-in-organic-arrangement is exactly frame 4's discovery.
Take: repeated identical units, pale cold luminosity, blackness with no floor.
Avoid: the creature read, tentacle silhouettes (constitution bans tentacle
clutter), motion.

## II. THE SINGLE SOURCE (Frame 3: one light at building scale)

**5. Fresnel lighthouse lens, lit, in a dark lamp room.**
Search: `first order Fresnel lens lit night lamp room`.
A manufactured light with visible internal geometry: concentric machined
facets, absolutely regular, quietly enormous.
Take: faceted internal structure, manufactured precision, glow with geometry.
Avoid: brass warmth, museum context, any steampunk read.

**6. Hot-air balloon envelope lit from inside at night.**
Search: `balloon glow night envelope inner structure`.
A large soft luminous body whose internal skeleton silhouettes through the
skin. This is how a single source can have anatomy without being organic.
Take: internal structure read through a glowing skin, scale against darkness.
Avoid: festival colour, crowds, multiple balloons.

## III. THE TETHERS (Frame 4: the first discrepancy)

**7. Deep-sea mooring lines and buoy tethers.**
Search: `oceanographic mooring line buoy underwater tether`.
A pale body held by one thin line that vanishes into black below. The whole
discrepancy in a single photograph.
Take: one thin dark line into depth, the implication of something far below.
Avoid: ocean blue-green, marine growth, daylight.

**8. Theatre fly-tower rigging seen from the grid.**
Search: `theatre fly system grid rigging lines counterweight`.
Dozens of parallel lines dropping from machinery into darkness, built for
silent raising and removal. Institutional, severe, and completely functional.
Take: parallel tethers as infrastructure, the sense that hanging things is a
JOB, geometry of lines converging on machinery.
Avoid: visible rope texture, stage warmth, any human figure.

## IV. THE ARMATURE (Frame 5: the reveal)

**9. Jacquard loom harness and heddle field.**
Search: `jacquard loom harness cords heddles industrial weaving`.
THE key reference of the whole board. Thousands of vertical cords descending
from a machine frame, every cord individually addressed by the mechanism
above. A machine whose entire purpose is holding thousands of threads and
deciding, per thread, what happens next.
Take: cords-to-mechanism convergence, per-unit addressability, the frame as a
dark horizon above the threads. Avoid: workshop clutter, wood warmth, cloth.

**10. Cable-laying ship drum and carousel.**
Search: `subsea cable laying ship carousel drum interior`.
Colossal spools of dark cable in industrial gloom, wound with mechanical
patience. The scale reference for the armature's spools.
Take: spool geometry at inhuman scale, matte black cable mass, patience.
Avoid: hi-vis jackets, daylight, sea.

**11. Mine headframe winding gear at night.**
Search: `colliery headframe winding wheel night`.
Black machinery above a shaft whose whole job is lowering and raising on
cables. Reads from a distance as architecture; understood, it is lifting gear.
Take: silhouette of duty-machinery against near-black sky, verticality.
Avoid: industrial-heritage romanticism, sunset.

**12. Turbine hall interiors, low light.**
Search: `power station turbine hall dark interior repeated machinery`.
A single room a kilometre long with the same dark machine repeated down its
length. The horizon-wide feeling the armature needs.
Take: one machine repeated to a vanishing point, low grazing light.
Avoid: control-room clutter, colour-coded pipes.

## V. THE REMOVAL (Frame 5: the procession)

**13. O. Winston Link's night photographs of working railways.**
Search: `O Winston Link night railroad photographs`.
The canonical images of dark machinery performing its duty at night: floodlit
process, deep blacks, nothing explained, nothing dramatised.
Take: the grade, flash-lit machinery against black, duty without spectacle.
Avoid: nostalgia, people, steam romance.

**14. Automated warehouse retrieval systems in dark aisles.**
Search: `automated storage retrieval system dark warehouse crane aisle`.
A carrier moving one unit through racking in near-darkness, indifferent and
precise. The procession is exactly this: removal as routine.
Take: one moving unit among thousands of stored ones, mechanical indifference.
Avoid: fulfilment-centre branding, safety yellow, cardboard.

## VI. THE SPENT SEDIMENT (Frames 5-6: what removal produces)

**15. Spent fuel racks in reactor cooling pools.**
Search: `spent fuel pool storage racks nuclear`.
Identical spent units racked in a dark grid underwater, still faintly potent.
The single most Dark Lattice image on this board, and the honest nuclear tie:
the site's fission feeling comes from here, not from glowing lines.
Take: gridded racking of retired units, dark water, quiet danger, the sense
that storage IS the monument. Avoid: the blue Cherenkov glow for now (colour
grammar is undecided), diagrams, hazard signage.

**16. Aircraft boneyards at dusk.**
Search: `Davis-Monthan aircraft boneyard aerial dusk`.
Thousands of identical retired bodies in rows fading to the horizon. Scale of
accumulated decommissioning.
Take: rows of the identical dead reaching the horizon, dusk flatness.
Avoid: desert warmth, patriotic framing, any single readable aircraft.

## VII. THE GRADE (all frames)

**17. JWST deep field, monochrome crops.**
Search: `JWST deep field grayscale crop`.
For value structure only: how many decades of brightness a black frame can
hold when the brightest thing is still small.
Take: the luminance distribution, blackness that reads rich not empty.
Avoid: colour, lensing arcs, recognisable press images.

## THE ANTI-BOARD — what this must never resemble

- **Immersive lantern and mirror rooms** (teamLab, Kusama-style infinity
  installations): the death read. If any frame could be photographed by a
  visitor holding a phone, it has failed.
- **Votive and devotional light walls**: banned vocabulary, banned imagery.
- **Chandelier art installations**: the recorded precedent; an earlier lattice
  died for exactly this read.
- **String lights, fairy lights, light festivals**: warmth, festivity, craft.
- **Cyberpunk server rooms and LED racks**: constitution 4.29, and the
  armature must predate every aesthetic, not reference one.
- **"Floating lanterns" and sky-lantern releases**: festival, release,
  upward hope. This world holds things; nothing in it is released upward.
- **AI-art glowing-orbs-in-a-dark-hall genre**: the exact slop this board
  exists to make impossible.
