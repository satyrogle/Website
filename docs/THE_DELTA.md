## 1. The whole thing in one sentence

The visitor enters a line, watches a future compute, changes one condition,
and then stands inside everything that changed.

## 2. The equation

```
X = baseline sequence
Y = one discrete intervention
Z = altered sequence − baseline sequence
```

In human terms:

> **WHAT WOULD HAPPEN + WHAT YOU CHANGED = THE WORLD BETWEEN THEM**

Beauty is not applied on top of the engine. Beauty is the shape the engine
produced. That is the whole claim, and it is why Z cannot be authored.

## 3. Three pages, one scene

Three separate narrative worlds; technically one continuous WebGL scene. No
loading, no fades, no scene replacement, no unrelated environments.

| Page | World                       | Verb      | Meaning                     |
| ---- | --------------------------- | --------- | --------------------------- |
| X    | Close, compressed, material | **scrub** | The system computes         |
| Y    | Deep, distributed, causal   | **alter** | The computation continues   |
| Z    | Vast, unfolded, inhabited   | **enter** | The difference is a place   |

The escalation is **OBJECT → EVENT → WORLD**.

## 4. X — THE FUTURE WITHOUT YOU

The camera crosses the hero's gold seam and discovers the line has thickness.
The hero remains intact behind the camera. No ring, no door frame, no new
monument.

Inside is one colossal black form built from ~40–60 compressed cross-sections
of the hero. Head on they overlap and read as a single monolith. From a slight
angle they resolve into successive states of the same system. The gold seam
runs through the entire stack as one continuous worldline.

**Interaction: scrub.** Vertical scroll moves through the deterministic
sequence. Each section shifts by its solved state, transfers pressure to the
next, and closes or exposes clearances. Revisiting reproduces the exact
movement; scrolling backward reverses it exactly. Pointer movement only
changes viewing angle to reveal the stack's thickness — it never drives the
simulation.

**The moment:** the visitor believes they are moving parts of an object, then
the sideways reveal shows they have been looking *through the thickness of a
computed future*.

> **THE SAME CAUSE MAKES THE SAME FUTURE.**

## 5. TICK ZERO — the hinge

At the end of X every state compresses into one aligned moment and a single
small internal blade is left accessible. This is the only place the visitor
may introduce a different condition.

Quiet and exact. No explosion, no pulse, no menu, no instruction panel.

Tick Zero is **promoted** from its old role as the floor of the ember fall. It
is now the boundary between deterministic observation and intervention.

## 6. Y — ONE CONDITION CHANGES

The blade has **three physical detents**: negative offset, neutral, positive
offset. Not a slider. The blade settles into a mechanical notch, so the input
is a deterministic selection rather than an approximate gesture.

Once selected, further scrolling runs the system forward again. Baseline and
altered futures initially occupy exactly the same space and read as one
object. They separate **only where the chosen condition produces a genuine
difference**.

> **No difference means no separation.**

Consequences propagate: a section moves fractionally, another takes a
different load, a clearance closes, a distant element crosses a threshold,
something behind the visitor changes without being touched.

**No clock.** The distant consequence occurs **further down the scroll**,
after a stretch where the visitor believes the reaction has ended. Scrolling
backward removes every consequence in reverse order; forward recreates the
same chain exactly.

**Gold behaviour.** Not a glowing network. One dominant causal line, visible
only where the altered sequence departs from the baseline. Its depth or width
may shift slightly with the magnitude of that difference, but it stays narrow
and restrained.

> **THE INTERVENTION IS CHOSEN. THE CONSEQUENCES ARE NOT.**

## 7. Z — STAND INSIDE THE DIFFERENCE

**Z does not extend in front of the camera. It unfolds around it.** This is a
structural rule, not a matter of art direction, and it is the correction that
made this version lockable.

At the end of Y, corresponding baseline and altered sections separate along
their individual difference vectors — some laterally, some vertically, some
diagonally, some barely at all. The field opens outward in multiple axes. The
camera stops travelling forward; the world expands from the visitor's
position.

The visitor is suspended inside an arrangement of hero-derived graphite
plates, openings and restrained structural elements. **Every gap is a real
computed difference:**

- small consequence — surfaces almost coincident, negative space compressed
- amplified consequence — surfaces separate, a large calm volume opens
- converging consequence — the forms approach again, contracting without ever
  becoming a corridor

The world's dimensions **are** the consequence map.

**Interaction: reveal.** Scroll controls how completely the field unfolds.
Pointer gives a restrained 5–8° orbit to inspect how the same geometry aligns
or separates. The visitor never flies through it like a game camera.

**The page stays inhabited. There is no pullback revealing a sculpture from
outside.** The closing image is the visitor suspended inside the completed
field with the gold causal seam running through the structure they caused.

> **THE DIFFERENCE HAS BECOME A PLACE.**

## 8. The non-negotiable Z frame test

This is a hard gate. **The Z keyframe is produced and judged before X or Y are
designed further, and before any `src/` work begins.**

**Z FAILS immediately if the frame contains:**

- a forward-facing tunnel mouth
- symmetrical side walls
- a visible floor route
- a strong central vanishing point
- one continuous curved shell
- a camera flying toward a destination

**Z PASSES when it reads as:**

- an open multi-axis field
- the visitor already inside it
- hero-derived rigid geometry
- vast negative space with no horizon
- one restrained causal seam
- a world unfolding around the camera rather than a route ahead of it
- **an arrangement with a rule in it, not scattered fragments**

That last criterion was added on review. With no floor, no horizon and no
vanishing point, suspended plates read as **debris** — an asteroid field of
slabs. The difference vectors are what make it structure rather than wreckage,
and that ordering has to be legible in a still image, not only in motion.

### 8.1 What the real Z keyframe must contain

Written after the first candidate failed (section 8A), because "not a tunnel"
turned out to be insufficient — an image can avoid every tunnel symptom and
still be an object you stand outside of.

- camera **physically embedded among** the plates
- large foreground forms **cropped beyond multiple frame edges**
- **no complete exterior silhouette**
- **no bilateral symmetry, no central crest axis**
- irregular gaps derived from the kernel's real output: seven visible
  differences across a 70× width range (`tools/delta-verify.mjs`)
- the gold seam seen **only in fragments through depth**, preferably off-centre
- negative space **continuing beyond the image**, never containing a finished
  object
- strong foreground occlusion and layered parallax, so it reads as somewhere
  the visitor is standing

### 8A The material reference — approved 2026-08-30

One frame was generated as a Z candidate and **failed the gate**. Jacob's own
verdict, recorded verbatim because the diagnosis is the useful part:

> "I mistook a strong, coherent object composition for an inhabited spatial
> composition. The centred seam, bilateral balance, complete silhouette and
> transparent presentation make it read as a crest being displayed — not a
> difference field surrounding the visitor."

It is retained, and approved, as the **plate-family and material reference
only**.

**It locks:**

- graphite plate stock and fracture language
- fine champagne-gold edge response
- the narrow causal-seam treatment
- surface detail and scale hierarchy
- the rule-based, not random-debris, quality

**It does not lock:**

- Z's composition
- camera placement
- plate positions
- symmetry
- spatial scale
- the final negative-space pattern

**A second failure mode was identified from it, and it now binds:** bilateral
symmetry is fatal to Z, and not merely on taste. Z's gaps are computed
differences — irregular positions, widths ranging 70×. **A difference field
cannot be symmetric.** A mirrored Z would be proof the geometry was authored
rather than derived, which collapses the entire claim the three pages exist to
make.

## 9. What was rejected on the way here, and why

Kept so none of it is re-proposed.

- **Rule → Consequence → Record.** Ended in an archive. An archive does not
  visibly equal X + Y, so Z was decoration with a thematic excuse.
- **The anamorphic sculpture.** Z as scattered elements resolving into one
  form from a single correct viewpoint. Killed by a contradiction at its
  centre: the elements could not both derive from the visitor's state *and* be
  guaranteed to resolve into a coherent composition. Either the form is
  authored — in which case the visitor did not produce it — or it is derived
  and nothing guarantees it looks like anything.
- **Z as a corridor between two walls.** Rejected because the concept does not
  protect the frame. "No tunnel" is a standing ban, and a justification that is
  intellectually correct does not stop an image reading as the banned thing.
  The fix had to be structural: unfold around the camera, not ahead of it.
- **A clock-based delayed consequence.** Broke the law that every state is a
  pure function of scroll position, in both directions. Replaced by distance
  down the scroll.
- **A continuous drag for the intervention.** Continuous input against baked
  outcomes would let a visitor feel the seams between families. Three detents
  read as *the system has states*, which is more deterministic, not less.

## 11. Input, devices and accessibility

The experience must be complete without a mouse.

- **Desktop** — scroll, plus drag on the three-position blade.
- **Mobile** — horizontal swipe or tap across the blade's three detents.
- **Keyboard** — left/right arrows to move between detents, Enter to confirm.

**Reduced motion** gets equivalent narrative states, never an empty version:

- camera travel removed
- X switches between fixed sequence states
- Y shows baseline and altered as stepped changes
- Z expands through a small number of stable compositions

The meaning survives when the animation does not.

## 12. Build reality

Four computed families:

1. baseline sequence
2. negative intervention sequence
3. neutral sequence
4. positive intervention sequence

For every state, store: position, rotation, difference magnitude, stress or
strain value, dominant causal-path value.

- **X** renders the baseline transforms.
- **Y** compares the selected intervention family against the baseline.
- **Z** takes those same difference vectors and unfolds the corresponding
  geometry around the camera.

Runtime is instanced geometry, transform interpolation, shader values and
camera control. **The heavy solving stays offline.** No particle field, no
procedural noise storm, no liquid morph, no portal ring, no architecture kit,
no large Meshy-generated environment.

Asset family, kept deliberately small:

1. one hero-derived vertical slice
2. three or four internal plate variants
3. one narrow recessed seam insert
4. one restrained structural connector
5. one inner graphite surface module
