# THREE VISUAL JOURNEYS

**Status: DECIDED, 2026-08-18. Jacob chose A, THE CONGREGATION.** It is the one
that reads as a system rather than a scene: a beautiful order whose hidden
logic is merciless. B, THE SLOW FIRE, ranked strongest pure spectacle and was
rejected for generic space-art risk. C, THE STILL SEA, was rejected as too
static to carry the homepage. B and C remain below as record, not options.

**Refinement applied to A at Jacob's instruction:** candle and cathedral
vocabulary is banned from the design language. Approved register: suspended
pale sources, hanging bone-white lights, stratified field of held luminosity.
The tether-to-armature reveal is the load-bearing element and stays subtle and
severe, never theatrical.
**Written:** 2026-08-18, after the art-direction-first workflow correction.
**Form:** Each journey is six frames: Opening, Approach, Descent, First
discrepancy, Hidden reveal, Return. Every frame describes what the visitor
SEES. The 30 banned patterns bind throughout; the UI in all three stays
restrained, typographic and serious, with real content at spatial stops in the
descent.

The three differ on every axis that matters: journey axis (vertical field /
radial shells / horizontal plane), temperature (cold bone / warm ash / neutral
silver), motion character (rare discrete events / one slow tide / absolute
stillness), and the kind of truth at the bottom (curation / consumption /
suspension).

---

## JOURNEY A — THE CONGREGATION

*The holiness is curation. Every light is placed, held, and removed when it
fails.*

### 1. Opening

Near-black. Filling the upper two thirds of the frame, a vast vertical field of
thousands of small bone-white lights hangs in deep space, arranged in loose
horizontal strata: a stratified field of held luminosity, suspended pale
sources seen from miles away. No two bands are evenly spaced. Faint cold haze sits between the bands and gives
them depth. The lower third of the frame is pure void. Everything is utterly
still; once in a long while, one light slowly brightens, as if breathing. The
wordmark and one line of company copy sit small in the lower left. It reads as
a stellar nursery, or a choir holding one silent chord. The awe is number and
arrangement.

### 2. Approach

Scroll draws us toward one region in the middle of the field. The strata begin
to separate in parallax: this is not a wall but a volume, layers behind layers.
Near lights slide out of the frame edges as we pass them. The haze thickens.
Differences appear that distance had hidden: some lights burn hard and clean,
others gutter. It is still beautiful. If anything, more so.

### 3. Descent

We are inside the field. Lights pass us slowly like snowfall lit from within.
Ahead, lanes of darkness run between columns of light; we travel along one.
The glow of the whole recedes behind us and the dark deepens ahead. At
intervals, in the empty lanes, company and project copy stands alone on the
void in plain type, nothing behind it, lit by nothing. Passing close to a
single light, we finally get scale: it is the size of a building, and its glow
has structure, faint facets, an internal geometry.

### 4. First discrepancy

The lights near us are identical. Not similar: identical, facet for facet, the
same posture, a manufactured regularity that nothing born ever has. And each
one is tethered. A hair-thin dark filament runs from the base of every light
down into the depth below. Some tethers hang slack and end at nothing. Those
lights are gone, and their absence, seen back up the way we came, forms the
quiet gaps in the strata that we had read as composition.

### 5. Hidden reveal

The tethers converge. Far below the field they gather into a colossal dark
armature: a horizon-wide rack of graphite-black arms, older and larger than
the field above it, each arm holding thousands of tether spools in perfect
rows. Along one arm, dimly, a slow procession is under way. A guttering light
is being drawn down, extinguished in transit, and racked into a sediment of
spent lights that stretches beyond the frame, thousands deep. Nothing hunts
us. Nothing notices us. The machine simply continues doing what it has clearly
done forever.

### 6. Return

Distance again, the full congregation restored, as beautiful as the first
frame. But the eye now finds what it missed: the faint mechanical regularity
of the strata; the low dark region at the base of the field that was in the
opening frame all along and never glowed; and one new gap, small and exact,
where a light we passed on the way down has since been removed. Nothing else
has changed. The chord is still being held. Now we know how.

### Why it cannot collapse into slop

Monochrome bone and graphite. No orb, no rings, no portal: a field and a rack.
The reveal is industrial and vast, not a creature. The Dark Lattice mapping is
one line: the beauty is a maintained record, and maintenance means removal.

## JOURNEY B — THE SLOW FIRE

*The light we worship is the burning of something that used to be a world.*

### 1. Opening

The right two thirds of the frame hold a vast radial formation: enormous soft
petals of luminous warm-white and ash-grey strata, layered around a brighter
heart, like a rose window built of smoke. The outer edges dissolve into black.
The heart's glow shifts at an almost imperceptible rate, the way firelight
does when seen from very far away. The left third is void, with the wordmark
and one line of copy low in it. It reads as a nebula, a celestial bloom, the
kind of image observatories publish. It is the warmest and most romantic of
the three openings.

### 2. Approach

We move toward the heart. The petals resolve into layered shells of stratified
ash, each shell a slightly different temperature of grey and gold, stacked
with gaps between them. We pass into the shadowed back of the outermost shell:
its outer face was luminous, its inner face is cold and dark. All light now
comes from ahead, through breaks in the layers.

### 3. Descent

We travel the gaps between shells. Stratified walls slide past on both sides,
enormous and slow; shafts of the heart's light cut across our path through
breaks; the space behind us closes into darkness. Fine ember motes drift past
us going the other way, outward, rising. At the shadowed stops between shells,
the company and project copy stands in plain type, lit faintly from one side
by the distant heart, like text read by firelight.

### 4. First discrepancy

The strata are not gas. Seen close, they carry right angles: window-like
voids, sheared straight edges, a broken grid too regular for any geology, all
of it charred. The rising motes that pass us are fragments, and every fragment
carries a piece of a right angle. Somewhere in this stretch the word for what
we are inside of quietly changes from cloud to plume.

### 5. Hidden reveal

The heart is not a star. It is an irregular shard the size of a civilisation:
a continent of near-black structure, cracked and layered, with one long
combustion front crawling across it, slow as a tide and completely silent.
Where the front passes, structure becomes light and ash and rises, and we
recognise the material of the petals we admired from outside. Ahead of the
front, the rest of the shard waits, intact and dark. The bloom is the plume of
this one long burn, and it is not finished.

### 6. Return

We are drawn back out through the shells to the opening distance. The
formation glows exactly as before. But the glow now reads as firelight, the
petals as smoke, and with attention it is just possible to see the heart
brightening along one edge, where the front is. A new petal is forming there.
The most beautiful part of the opening frame turns out to be the newest smoke.

### Why it cannot collapse into slop

The reveal is a shard, never a sphere, so no planet and no orb. The shells are
broken and irregular, so no rings. Heat exists only where something actually
burns, which is the constitution's own rule for warm colour. It is the closest
of the three to the galaxy-and-dying-world instinct, delivered without either.

## JOURNEY C — THE STILL SEA

*The calm is suspension. Nothing has been allowed to finish falling, and
underneath lies everything that already did.*

### 1. Opening

Horizontal, where the other two are vertical and radial. We hover high above
an endless pale luminous surface stretching to a black horizon: a moonlit
cloud-sea, or an ocean of mercury, absolutely still, without one ripple. Above
it, pure void, no stars. The glow is soft and sourceless, as if the surface
itself were the only light left anywhere. The wordmark sits in the upper left
void. Nothing moves at all. It is the most restrained opening of the three,
and its awe is the awe of an enormous held breath.

### 2. Approach

We descend vertically. The surface refuses to ripple as we near it; instead
its sheen breaks into grain. It is not liquid. The sea is an infinitely dense
suspension of minute pale things holding one perfect plane, like a snowfall
compressed into a single sheet of altitude and then stopped.

### 3. Descent

We pass through the surface, and it closes over us as a glowing ceiling. All
around, millions of small pale fragments hang mid-fall, absolutely motionless,
a blizzard with time removed. The ceiling light fades as we sink; darkness
rises to meet us from below. The descent stops happen in clearings, pockets
where nothing hangs, and there the company and project copy stands alone in
the dark, the only upright thing in a world of arrested falling.

### 4. First discrepancy

The fragments near us have broken edges. They are pieces of things: sheared
faces, torn structure, corners of something that was once assembled. Every one
of them points downward, mid-fall, held by nothing. And they are sorted, fine
above and heavy below, the way sediment sorts. Once, exactly once, a fragment
slips a few metres and stops again. The stillness is not peace. It is hold.

### 5. Hidden reveal

Beneath the suspension, the floor. The hanging blizzard ends at a lightless
bed: strata upon strata of settled fragments compacted into layered rock,
kilometres deep, each layer a generation of things that were finally released
and allowed to land. Faint seams of the surface's pale glow run pressed
between the strata. The scale inverts everything above: the luminous sea we
began with is only the thinnest, newest film on top of what has already been
let go. Most of everything is down here.

### 6. Return

We rise back through the held blizzard and break the surface from beneath. The
still sea again, serene to the horizon, exactly as the opening. But the calm
now reads as suspension, the glow as the light of the most recent layer, and
just before the frame settles, far off toward the horizon, one region of the
surface lowers by a hair, silently, and is still again. The sea admits, once,
what it is.

### Why it cannot collapse into slop

There is no object anywhere in it: a plane, a depth, and a floor. Monochrome
silver and black. The horizontal axis and total stillness match no tech-site
reference in the library. And the reveal is the most Dark Lattice of the
three: under every calm surface, the compacted record of everything that was
ever let fall.
