# Reference study: CONTROL (Remedy, 2019)

Commissioned by Jacob, 2026-08-30, while judging the Z blockout: "do some
research on Control, they achieved something like this." Per the standing
law, a reference names QUALITIES to extract, never technologies to
implement. Control is a study for THE DELTA's Z; it is not a build target.

Why it is the right reference: the Oldest House is a brutalist monolith
whose interior breaks, shifts and floats — monumental rectilinear masses,
one accent colour, suspended debris, no horizon. That is Z's exact
problem, solved at AAA scale, and their answers transfer.

## What they actually did

**The mundane before the chaos.** World design director Stuart Macdonald:
the mundanity of the space matters "because 10 seconds later everything's
going to be trashed... we play on that contrast." The break reads because
the whole was believable first.

**Clean geometry is what makes destruction readable.** Narrative designer
Brooke Maggs: brutalism's straight, clean lines work well when the player
destroys things — a simple environment gives way to detailed effects
without overwhelming the eye. Their masses ARE boxes. They read as
monumental through proportion, repetition-with-variation and light — not
through detailing the boxes away.

**Debris is chunks, like a jigsaw, and every chunk is a rigid body.**
Northlight pre-divides walls into chunks; fragments are treated as rigid
bodies where other engines fake them. Three tiers of scale: slab, chunk,
fine rubble. The fine tier is what makes the big tiers feel huge.

**The Astral Plane: monochrome masses, a few gold sprinkles, a void.**
Floating geometric structures in an endless void, mostly axis-aligned,
sparse rotation, and ONE constant landmark (the black pyramid) always
visible for orientation. Stillness reads as HELD — authority, not rubble.

**The Ashtray Maze: architecture moves with intent.** Walls fold and stow
along clean axes, with momentum and a dead stop. Reconfiguration reads as
a mechanism operating, never as debris drifting.

## STEAL (for Z)

1. **Contrast as the weapon.** X must read as whole, ordered, believable
   architecture so Z's break means something. This is already THE DELTA's
   structure — Control confirms the emphasis: spend the effort making X
   read REAL.
2. **Do not fear the boxes.** The blockout's "building blocks" fault is
   proportion, hierarchy and light — not the rectilinear language itself.
   Keep flat monumental faces; fix scale distribution and lighting.
3. **Three tiers of debris scale.** Slab → chunk → fine chips. The
   blockout has two. The third, smallest tier is what sells the mass of
   the first. Every fragment stays a rigid body — already our law.
4. **Rotational restraint.** Most departed pieces near-aligned; tilt the
   FEW. A field where everything tumbles is rubble; a field where almost
   everything is held level, and two pieces lean, is authority. Matches
   the embalmed-world law exactly.
5. **One constant landmark.** Their pyramid is our SEAM — the one line
   always visible for orientation in Z. Already in the direction; treat
   it as load-bearing, not decoration.
6. **Departure with intent.** Pieces translate along their vector and
   stop dead. No drift, no wobble, no idle motion. Matches
   pure-function-of-scroll.
7. **(Optional, derived, cheap)** Departed pieces carry gold on the face
   that used to press against the seam — the gilded cleft face leaves
   with the piece. Orientation cue and continuity, derived from where
   the piece actually stood.

## SLOP IF COPIED

- The white void. Ours is dark; the inversion is the brand.
- The pyramid as an OBJECT. A floating landmark object is the banned
  "cool 3D object"; our landmark is a line.
- Hiss red, maroon wallpaper, any literal Oldest House interior.
- The origami fold as animation flourish. Their walls fold because a
  mechanism operates; copying the fold without the mechanism is
  decoration.
- Destruction as spectacle. Control's chaos is combat; Z is the
  AFTERMATH, embalmed. We steal the broken state, never the explosion.
- Runtime physics destruction (PhysX-style). Technology is chosen last;
  our positions are solved offline by the kernel. Their fragments react;
  ours are computed.
