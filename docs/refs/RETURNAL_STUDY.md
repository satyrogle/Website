# Reference study: RETURNAL (Housemarque, 2021) — and two link verdicts

Commissioned with the godmode build order, 2026-08-30. Same law as the
Control study: qualities to extract, never technologies to implement.

## What they actually did

**Atmosphere as a material.** Atropos reads through volumetric depth —
fog, drizzle, light shafts, spore-laden air. The ruins are colossal and
broken, but it is the AIR that makes them read as a place. This is the
exact term CLAUDE.md records as missing from every frame this project
has produced.

**Behaviour drives visuals, minimal prebake.** Their NGP system's
philosophy: simulate at runtime, let simulation DATA drive every visual
element (fluid sim feeding particles, vegetation, fog). The philosophy —
not the tech — is our own "the mechanism is the form."

**Timing telegraphs state.** Tentacle animation timing was iterated with
the enemy team so motion communicates system state before anything else
does. For us: Y's choreography of departures IS the reading of causality
— order and pacing are information, never garnish.

**Dark xeno-monumentality with few living lights.** Cold ruins, restrained
bioluminescent accents. Maps directly onto graphite + one gold.

## STEAL

1. **Atmosphere pass on the delta world**: layered depth-cueing, a faint
   shaft down the cleft, dark air with body. The missing physical term.
2. **Data-driven display**: every visual layer in X/Y/Z keyed to kernel
   values (offset, gap, strain), nothing keyed to taste except stated
   display constants.
3. **Choreography as telegraphy**: the order in which pieces move is the
   explanation of causality; protect it, never decorate over it.
4. **Few living lights**: the seam, the torn gold faces, nothing else.

## SLOP IF COPIED

- Particle storms and bullet-hell density (their combat language).
- Organic/tentacle/biolum creature vocabulary.
- Fluid simulation for its own sake — technology is chosen last.
- The crimson-wastes palette.

## Link verdicts (Jacob asked "can you use these in any way?")

**originkit.dev — NO.** A free/pro animated UI component pack: Pulsating
Border, Neon Border, Starfield Button, Plasma Ring, Glass Icon. This is
the banned category three times over: designer mega-packs (rejected in
CLAUDE.md), glowing borders everywhere (reject list), and a UI library
(locked out of the stack). Nothing to extract — its whole catalogue is
what this site defines itself against.

**particles.casberry.in — NO for the build.** An AI particle-swarm toy:
20k particles forming text, uploaded models and blueprints, with glow
controls and a community gallery. Charming, but particles-forming-a-shape
is the canonical "particle demo" — a kill word here — and the
no-decorative-particles law covers the rest. One idea worth keeping as a
NOTE only: its GLB/OBJ-to-point-anchor import is a tidy authoring trick
if a future feature ever needs real point anchors from a mesh. Not now.
