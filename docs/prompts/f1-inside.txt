Standing among pale silver-gold plants that rise far above head height, their slender stalks and heavy feathered seed heads filling the lower half of the frame and continuing past the top edge. The plants themselves are the only source of light: they glow from within, softly incandescent, brightest in wide irregular patches that fade outward into deep gold and then into black, with no beam, no shaft and no sun anywhere. Between and behind them the field goes on and on, dissolving into a still wall of pale luminous haze that has no horizon, no ground and no sky, only depth. The upper corners and the outer edges of the frame are pure black. Nothing else is present: no figure, no creature, no path, no structure, no visible light source, no text. Anamorphic cinema still, deep blacks, immense scale, absolutely still.
