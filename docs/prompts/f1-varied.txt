Looking down the length of an immense dark field from inside it. The plants are wildly unlike one another: some enormous and heavy headed, some thin and bare, some bent almost double, some broken and hanging, of every height from ankle to far above the frame, and they grow in irregular clumps separated by wide empty voids of black ground where nothing grows at all. In the near foreground one or two stand huge and razor sharp. Behind them the field falls away for miles, the clumps becoming smaller and sparser and darker with distance until far off they are only scattered specks of gold, and beyond even those a single faint band of pale light lies across the far distance and nothing else. Almost all of the field is dark. Light lives inside the plants themselves and pools in only two or three wide areas, softly incandescent there and falling away rapidly to deep gold and then to black; there is no beam, no shaft, no sun and no visible source anywhere. No horizon, no sky. On the far left and far right sheer black faceted stone, flat and untextured, holds the outer edges in pure black. No figure, no creature, no path, no structure, no text. Anamorphic cinema still, deep blacks, immense scale, absolutely still.
