A vast field of golden rice paddy at dusk, seen from just above the heads of the plants, filling the frame from edge to edge and reaching into the far distance. Cutting through the middle of the picture is a wedge of absolute darkness: its point at the bottom centre where the viewer stands, its two edges opening outward to the top corners, so the field is visible only in the lower left and lower right. Inside the wedge there is nothing but black. A low warm light from behind the field makes every grain head burn gold while the stems fall to deep amber, and the far field dissolves into warm haze under a black sky. No sun, no figures, no path, no buildings, no text. Cinematic still in the manner of 300, anamorphic, deep blacks, holy stillness.
