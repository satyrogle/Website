Looking down the length of an immense field of pale silver-gold plants, from inside it. In the near foreground a few plants stand enormous and razor sharp, taller than the frame, their feathered seed heads close enough to touch. Behind them the same plants repeat smaller and smaller and smaller into a vast distance, thousands of them, until far away they are no larger than sparks and merge into one faint continuous band of gold suspended in blackness. The plants are the only light: they glow from within, softly incandescent, brightest in wide irregular patches that fade outward to deep gold and then to black, with no beam, no shaft, no sun and no visible source. There is no horizon, no sky and no visible ground, only the field receding forever into darkness. On the far left and far right, sheer black faceted stone, absolutely flat and without texture, frames the view and holds the outer third of the frame in pure black. Nothing else is present: no figure, no creature, no path, no structure, no text. Anamorphic cinema still, deep blacks, immense scale, absolutely still.
