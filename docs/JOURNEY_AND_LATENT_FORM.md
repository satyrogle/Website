## The law of the journey

The Field Engine exists to reveal one truth:

> Beneath a surface of magnificent indifference, something enormous is already
> aware of the visitor.

The visitor does not cause that truth and the world does not transform into a
different story. Interaction reveals what was already present. The journey is a
change in understanding, not a threat arriving.

## Emotional register

- **Prague in monsoon:** wet, old, magnificent, indifferent. Never melancholy.
- **Comfortably Numb:** restrained and almost flat on the surface, with
  something immense moving underneath.
- **Anhedonia:** pleasure is withheld at the surface without becoming sadness.
  The crossing's beauty matters because it breaks through that restraint once,
  without turning into reward spectacle or triumph.
- **Sinister:** knowing awareness. The discomfort is inadequacy — the sense
  that one could make, think, or work better — not fear of being harmed.
- **Blue hour:** light fades, but twilight always lives. Nothing resolves into
  crushed black.

## The journey

1. **Magnificent indifference.** The Split Spire appears holy, remote, and
   complete. The world does not welcome, explain, or perform for the visitor.
2. **Discrepancy.** The watcher attends before the visitor acts. The ledger
   measures. Retained, struck, and culled read as standards and verdicts. The
   seam suggests depth without behaving like a portal.
3. **The crossing soars.** Passing the seam is the most beautiful and expansive
   beat of the site. Light moves past the visitor and the apparent boundary
   opens into a world impossibly larger than the exterior. Beauty comes before
   comprehension.
4. **The shadow road.** A world-space shadow crosses the luminous interior. It
   is cast by the latent presence and encountered before its source can be
   read. It is evidence and direction, never pursuit, attack, or a tunnel.
5. **The latent form.** At the end, the presence is dark, faceless, backlit,
   still, and already attentive. It does not arrive, awaken, praise, condemn,
   chase, or threaten. The visitor understands: **it had already seen them.**
   What first felt sinister becomes a demand the visitor places on themselves.
6. **The return.** The opening returns almost exactly as it was. Understanding
   has changed more than the world. At most one visible world difference may
   remain; the exact session ledger may record what happened.

## What was latent from the first frame

| Seen at the opening | First reading | Understanding at the end |
|---|---|---|
| the Split Spire | a holy, self-contained monument | a boundary through which a larger reality was glimpsed |
| the watcher | an ornamental or reactive detail | awareness that preceded the visitor's action |
| the ledger | austere system telemetry | a record of standards, attempts, and verdicts |
| retained / struck / culled | technical status language | a bar: what endured, what was marked, what was not good enough |
| the fissure | a beautiful source of light | the seam through which concealed depth became visible |
| the interior shadow | an absence of light | evidence of the latent presence before it could be seen |

Nothing at the end may depend on a new explanation that was absent from the
opening. The ending earns its meaning by re-reading existing evidence.

## The latent-form contract

This chapter decides the form's meaning, not its exact silhouette. Shape,
material, camera, and rendering remain gated by approved visual reference.

The approved image must preserve all of these constraints:

- dark, faceless, backlit, still;
- readable as an enormous presence without becoming a boss or creature reveal;
- awareness without an eye construction or facial expression;
- no humanoid hero pose, lunging limb, restraint, prison, or containment
  failure;
- no jump scare, attack, pursuit, moral verdict, praise, or condemnation;
- no complete explanatory view; recognition comes from accumulated evidence.

The latent form is not evil. It is not kind. It is aware, and it does not need
the visitor's permission or response.

## Test of success

The intended afterimage is:

> It was beautiful. Then I realised it knew I was there — and I wanted to
> become better.

The journey has failed if it is merely darker, if the seam reads as a portal,
if the shadow reads as pursuit, if the latent form reads as a monster, or if
the visitor remembers a reveal but cannot say what earlier evidence made it
inevitable.
