# Identity — the board is the identity

**2026-09-03. Jacob lifted the bans on visual identity.** It is his
company and his brain, and the site is to reflect what is in it. This
file exists so a later session does not read the older documents and
quietly re-impose what he removed.

The reference board — chakra and energy-body figures, the caduceus, the
eye in the pyramid, the Tree of Life over the Vitruvian figure,
Leonardo's exploded mechanisms, annotated anatomical plates, the
Penrose diagram, whiteboards of equations, the textbook pile, the books
on mastery and consciousness — is one visual tradition: the diagram as
a way of knowing. Energy along an axis, the body as a mechanism, the
observer drawn inside the geometry. That is the site's visual language.

## How it is applied

**Into the guided journey, where it already is. Not as new pages.**

Recorded because it was got wrong once: a separate plate page was built
on 2026-09-03 and removed the same day. Jacob asked whether the themes
could strengthen the site he has; a second page is not that, whatever
it looks like. The journey is the site. The themes go into its
stations, its world, its margins and its copy.

Standing so far: three marginalia beside X, Y and Z (`src/glyphs.css`,
inline in `index.html`) — the future cone, the eye in the pyramid, the
observer inside the geometry. They sit in the left channel above each
