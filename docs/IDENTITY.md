# Identity — the board is the identity

**2026-09-03. Jacob lifted the bans on visual identity.** His words:
it is his company, his brain, and the site is to reflect what is going
on in it. This file exists so the next session does not read the older
documents and quietly re-impose what he removed.

The reference board — chakra and energy-body figures, the caduceus,
the eye in the pyramid, the Tree of Life over the Vitruvian figure,
Leonardo's exploded mechanisms, annotated anatomical plates, the
Penrose diagram, whiteboards of equations, the textbook pile, the
books on mastery and consciousness — is not a list of things to avoid.
It is one visual tradition: the diagram as a way of knowing. Energy
along an axis, the body as a mechanism, the observer drawn inside the
geometry. That is the site's visual language now.

What still binds, because it is not taste:

- copy states only what the studio can substantiate;
- no external requests; fonts stay self-hosted;
- one simulation, one authoritative state; renderers observe;
- the seed decides the world; nothing on a plate is drawn by hand.

First projection: `record.html`, Plate I — the kernel as the figure
(`src/plate/Plate.ts`), and the station glyphs on the journey page.
Older documents that describe bans on figures, eyes, symbols, extra
colour or "cool objects" are superseded on those points by this file.
