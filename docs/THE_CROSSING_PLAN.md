## 1. Journey and Latent Form (AUTHORITATIVE)

The complete chapter is `docs/JOURNEY_AND_LATENT_FORM.md`. Its governing law is:

> Beneath a surface of magnificent indifference, something enormous is already
> aware of the visitor.

The opening is wet, old, holy, and indifferent. The crossing is the site's most
beautiful and expansive beat. A shadow cast by the latent presence leads across
the luminous interior. At the end, the presence is dark, faceless, backlit,
still, and already aware. The return changes the visitor's understanding more
than the world.

**Sinister means knowing awareness and inadequacy, not threat.** The watcher is
the thesis; the ledger measures; retained, struck, and culled are standards and
verdicts. No monster, chained thing, prison, containment failure, escape drama,
jump scare, chase, or attack is part of this story.

The chapter decides meaning but refuses to invent the exact latent-form shape.
That remains gated by an approved V6 image. Visible placeholders and contact
sheets remain permanently dropped.

## 5. GATE E0 — EXTERIOR CORRECTION (work order)

The landing must establish magnificent indifference, not ceremony. A
processional approach makes the world appear arranged for the visitor, which
means the opposite of what it must mean. These five strikes are Jacob's,
confirmed 2026-08-22.

### E0.1 Steps and plinth: DELETE

Delete the 13 treads, the three tiers, the raised platform. Do not replace with
fewer/larger steps, a circular basin, a ring, or any podium.

**Replacement — the ground was forced around the object, not built to present
it:**

- flush intersection of both blades with the plain;
- one narrow, **asymmetric** subsidence slot at each blade root;
- deep contact shadow;
- very restrained continuation of the fissure light below grade.

**Scale without stairs** (the treads were the only human ruler, so this is
load-bearing): viewer-height camera, large readable surface courses on the
blades, sparse tiny cold points near the base, atmospheric separation between
hero and choir, the width and depth of the blade roots, strong near/far parallax
on approach.

**Coupled work, in scope, not scope creep:**

- The **cold pool** (sinister gate 4) currently lives partly on plinth and tread
  surfaces. Re-express it entirely on the ground lane and the new subsidence
  slots. The cold-arrival meaning survives; its host surfaces change.
- The **press floor law** is currently "the plinth's top lip (6.4 + CELL)".
  Re-floor it to the new contact so no press seats where it cannot be seen.
- **Scree and pylon fragments: delete** with the rest of the forecourt dressing.

### E0.2 Crown halo: DELETE the disk

Remove the independent circular halo read entirely — do not merely lower its
opacity. Replace with vertically elongated atmospheric lift behind the blades:
narrow, slightly irregular, never perfectly centred, no radial falloff. Let lid
and draw carry the concentration. Blade tips must stay legible against it.

The read is *the sky is under pressure around this object*, not *this object has
an aura*. The fissure remains the only unequivocally bright element.

**Not touched:** the sky break's own glow (sinister gate 3) stays exactly as
approved. Only the halo sprite dies.

### E0.3 Corrosion: REBUILD THE HIERARCHY

Keep the disease. Reject the current even, all-over, high-contrast read (it
looks like decorative marbling or a contour map).

**Target coverage, measured as screen-space coverage at the landing camera** —
not UV-mask coverage:

| band | share |
|---|---|
| clean graphite | 72-78% |
| ghost residue (visible only under raking light) | 12-16% |
| secondary outbreaks (readable webbing + matrix residue) | 7-10% |
| dense disease | 3-5% |

**Distribution:** one dominant outbreak carrying diagonal motion but never a
clean slash; 4-6 secondary outbreaks; many faint residual traces; both blades
affected asymmetrically; no mirrored placement; no continuous all-over network;
large untouched intervals.

**Reading order at landing:** fissure first, silhouette second, disease third or
fourth. **At close range:** roughness variation, pitted/webbed structure, sparse
matrix pinpricks, directional residue, shallow relief. **Must remain
comprehensible with emission disabled.**

### E0.4 Choir: REOPEN GEOMETRY, PRESERVE COMPOSITION

Silhouette-sheet approval did not guarantee the in-scene meshes reproduced those
silhouettes. They currently read as repeated tapered blocks.

**Preserve:** landing camera, existing placement coordinates, distance
hierarchy, the landing-camera alignment plane, fog/depth relationship.

**Reopen:** mesh silhouettes, width-to-height ratios, crown shapes, lower-body
massing, negative spaces, depth profiles.

**Required distinct roles:** one broad low Buttress, one severe Cleaver, one
broken/voided Split Mass, one broad Recessed Wall, one distant Far Witness, one
heavy Raked Bastion. **The Overhang is permanently dead.**

No two masses may share base profile, taper, crown angle, or height/width ratio
with only a scale change. Judge new geometry at current placement first; move
masses only if the silhouette pass proves placement is the remaining issue.

Note: `choir.py`'s alignment plane is solved **from the landing camera**. The
landing camera does not move in this plan. Do not move it.

### E0.5 Forecourt: worn axis and pylons DIE, ruin LIVES, contact LIVES

- **Worn axis: delete.** A channel ground in by ten thousand approaches is an
  invitation, which is what E0 exists to kill. Remove both halves (albedo
  darkening and the roughness/polish term).
- **Pylons: delete.** A pair flanking an axis is ceremony architecture.
- **Ruin: keep**, re-authored as broken ground and collapsed mass rather than
  ruined stairs, since the stairs it was ruining no longer exist. Wreckage reads
  as consequence, not invitation.
- **The contact (fracture network + charge): keep.** Jacob's only unprompted
  praise in the build. It is physical evidence that the Spire changes the field
  around it and visual evidence of the system's measured standard. It is not
  containment damage and not decorative dressing.

2. The hero intersects the world directly and reads as embedded.
3. No circular halo disk is perceptible.
4. The fissure is still the brightest visual event.
5. Most of the hero is clean graphite.
6. Corruption exists in several places with an obvious hierarchy.
7. The choir read as structurally different masses, not one tower rescaled.
8. Landing camera and typography unchanged.
9. No interior system, crossing, station or latent-form work was touched.
10. One desktop capture presented (plus one mobile), then **stop**.

Proof: desktop + mobile still. Inspection: **rule B** — one look against 1-7,

**World space, not screen space** — it must affect terrain, haze, entry lights

**Forbidden:** tunnel meshes, tubes, rings, lens-only fakery, a black road

## 7. Global execution rules

**Environment.** Work in this checkout on `claude/genesis`, from the HEAD
carrying the six sinister gates. Dev server: launch entry `genesis-node` (the
npm bin is broken here). Typecheck:
`node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json`.

**Token discipline is part of correctness.** Under rule A, never read a captured
PNG back into context — capture, state the path, stop. No tuning loops, no
re-capture cycles, no extra verification passes. `tools/quality.mjs` runs once at
the end of a gate. `src/render/JourneyRenderer.ts` is thousands of lines: grep
for the anchor comment, read only the surrounding range.

**Determinism.** No `Math.random` in world logic. New seeded streams use
`mulberry32(seed ^ <new salt>)`. Never reorder an existing stream — it changes
the whole world.

**Capture discipline.** Shipping page, never `?bare=1` (DOM scrims change the
frame). Pointer untouched, so the authored pose and the pre-pointer watcher
state are what is photographed. Jacob's viewport is 2270x1278.

**Traps already paid for — do not rediscover:**
- Canvas pixels are only true inside `requestAnimationFrame`.
- Playwright `evaluate` takes **one** argument; a stringified function silently
  returns undefined.
- The bloom threshold (0.78) decides visibility: a front below it is real in a
  difference image and invisible on the monitor.
- Anything standing on the plain stays under distance 2400 or it hovers.
- Objects and ground must fog toward the same colour at the same distance.
- Mean-absolute-frame-diff is blind to low-contrast motion. Look at two frames.
- Headless/SwiftShader is not GPU truth.
- Every tuned value belongs to a camera pose. The landing camera does not move.

**Untouchable during interior work:** the six sinister gates' mechanics where
they comply with `docs/JOURNEY_AND_LATENT_FORM.md`, the landing camera,
typography, and the DOM content contract. Superseded containment causality and
the old falling-luminance crossing assertion are not protected.
