## 1. The story (LOCKED — do not reopen)

**The monument is not a temple. It is a lock.**

The holy exterior is the restraint. The light in the fissure is what leaks from
the thing being held. Everything already built re-means itself:

- The **ties and lattice** were never holding the tower up. They hold the thing
  inside *down*.
- The **ledger** is the containment log. RETAINED / STRUCK / CULLED is
  maintenance of a seal, not telemetry.
- The **culls** are strain damage. Something inside pulled; a piece of the wall
  let go. What read as decay at the landing was containment failure.
- The **watcher** was never decoration. Something noticed the visitor before
  the visitor did anything.

**Agency lock.** The held thing may *notice* the visitor visually. The
containment system owns the ledger. `OBSERVER NOTED — RETAINED` is written by
the containment, once, unprompted, near the encounter. Never repeated.

**Strain causality — one authoritative chain, never a decoupled effect:**

```
true-form strain -> containment load rises -> one exterior cell fails
  -> ledger records CULLED -> return frame retains the evidence
```

**The true form is not a character.** No humanoid colossus, no boss silhouette,
no face in the fissure, no limbs, no mechanical skeleton. It is a restrained
spatial mass: taut members vanishing into dark, one large mass that never fully
resolves, impossible negative space under load, one small strain motion, the
rest obscured. Horrifying because the restraints imply force and the scale is
unresolved — never because something is shown.

**Dropped permanently:** the sky break as an escape scar (over-explains; the lid
stays structural evidence of enclosure, the draw stays the hero acting on the
atmosphere). The white-out crossing. The lattice-as-reader reading. Visible
placeholder true forms. Contact-sheet reference selection.

## 5. GATE E0 — EXTERIOR CORRECTION (work order)

The landing was approved under the *temple* story. Under the *lock* story a
processional approach means the opposite of what it must mean. These five
strikes are Jacob's, confirmed 2026-08-22.

### E0.1 Steps and plinth: DELETE

Delete the 13 treads, the three tiers, the raised platform. Do not replace with
fewer/larger steps, a circular basin, a ring, or any podium.

**Replacement — the ground was forced around the object, not built to present
it:**

- flush intersection of both blades with the plain;
- one narrow, **asymmetric** subsidence slot at each blade root;
- deep contact shadow;
- very restrained continuation of the fissure light below grade.

**Scale without stairs** (the treads were the only human ruler, so this is
load-bearing): viewer-height camera, large readable surface courses on the
blades, sparse tiny cold points near the base, atmospheric separation between
hero and choir, the width and depth of the blade roots, strong near/far parallax
on approach.

**Coupled work, in scope, not scope creep:**

- The **cold pool** (sinister gate 4) currently lives partly on plinth and tread
  surfaces. Re-express it entirely on the ground lane and the new subsidence
  slots. The cold-arrival meaning survives; its host surfaces change.
- The **press floor law** is currently "the plinth's top lip (6.4 + CELL)".
  Re-floor it to the new contact so no press seats where it cannot be seen.
- **Scree and pylon fragments: delete** with the rest of the forecourt dressing.

### E0.2 Crown halo: DELETE the disk

Remove the independent circular halo read entirely — do not merely lower its
opacity. Replace with vertically elongated atmospheric lift behind the blades:
narrow, slightly irregular, never perfectly centred, no radial falloff. Let lid
and draw carry the concentration. Blade tips must stay legible against it.

The read is *the sky is under pressure around this object*, not *this object has
an aura*. The fissure remains the only unequivocally bright element.

**Not touched:** the sky break's own glow (sinister gate 3) stays exactly as
approved. Only the halo sprite dies.

### E0.3 Corrosion: REBUILD THE HIERARCHY

Keep the disease. Reject the current even, all-over, high-contrast read (it
looks like decorative marbling or a contour map).

**Target coverage, measured as screen-space coverage at the landing camera** —
not UV-mask coverage:

| band | share |
|---|---|
| clean graphite | 72-78% |
| ghost residue (visible only under raking light) | 12-16% |
| secondary outbreaks (readable webbing + matrix residue) | 7-10% |
| dense disease | 3-5% |

**Distribution:** one dominant outbreak carrying diagonal motion but never a
clean slash; 4-6 secondary outbreaks; many faint residual traces; both blades
affected asymmetrically; no mirrored placement; no continuous all-over network;
large untouched intervals.

**Reading order at landing:** fissure first, silhouette second, disease third or
fourth. **At close range:** roughness variation, pitted/webbed structure, sparse
matrix pinpricks, directional residue, shallow relief. **Must remain
comprehensible with emission disabled.**

### E0.4 Choir: REOPEN GEOMETRY, PRESERVE COMPOSITION

Silhouette-sheet approval did not guarantee the in-scene meshes reproduced those
silhouettes. They currently read as repeated tapered blocks.

**Preserve:** landing camera, existing placement coordinates, distance
hierarchy, the landing-camera alignment plane, fog/depth relationship.

**Reopen:** mesh silhouettes, width-to-height ratios, crown shapes, lower-body
massing, negative spaces, depth profiles.

**Required distinct roles:** one broad low Buttress, one severe Cleaver, one
broken/voided Split Mass, one broad Recessed Wall, one distant Far Witness, one
heavy Raked Bastion. **The Overhang is permanently dead.**

No two masses may share base profile, taper, crown angle, or height/width ratio
with only a scale change. Judge new geometry at current placement first; move
masses only if the silhouette pass proves placement is the remaining issue.

Note: `choir.py`'s alignment plane is solved **from the landing camera**. The
landing camera does not move in this plan. Do not move it.

### E0.5 Forecourt: worn axis and pylons DIE, ruin LIVES, contact LIVES

- **Worn axis: delete.** A channel ground in by ten thousand approaches is an
  invitation, which is what E0 exists to kill. Remove both halves (albedo
  darkening and the roughness/polish term).
- **Pylons: delete.** A pair flanking an axis is ceremony architecture.
- **Ruin: keep**, re-authored as broken ground and collapsed mass rather than
  ruined stairs, since the stairs it was ruining no longer exist. Wreckage reads
  as consequence, not invitation.
- **The contact (fracture network + charge) : keep.** Jacob's only unprompted
  praise in the build, and under the lock story it is the containment-strain
  evidence on the plain, wired to the culls. It is not decorative dressing.

### E0 acceptance — all ten must be true

1. No repeated treads and no ceremonial approach remain.
2. The hero intersects the world directly and reads as embedded.
3. No circular halo disk is perceptible.
4. The fissure is still the brightest visual event.
5. Most of the hero is clean graphite.
6. Corruption exists in several places with an obvious hierarchy.
7. The choir read as structurally different masses, not one tower rescaled.
8. Landing camera and typography unchanged.
9. No interior system, crossing, station or true-form work was touched.
10. One desktop capture presented (plus one mobile), then **stop**.

Proof: desktop + mobile still. Inspection: **rule B** — one look against 1-7,
at most one correction, hand over.

## 6. Remaining gates

### I1 — THE BRACE
Committing to the mouth, three synchronized cues: camera sway stops, the
fissure's micro-instability goes perfectly steady, surface micro-activity
freezes. A held breath, no spectacle. Drive the existing stillness signal from
scroll proximity as well as idle. `__dl.still(0|1)` toggle for live A/B.
**Forbidden:** a cull at the threshold (distracts, pre-explains the damage), the
light gathering to a point (makes it a portal), touching input parallax.
Proof: short A/B clip. Inspection: A.

### I2 — THE SWALLOW
Entering the slit: the seam's light slides to the frame edges and passes around
the camera while darkness opens in the centre and takes the screen. Pass
*through* the light into the dark.
**Hard rules:** no full-screen white; no frame brighter than the landing seam;
no closed glowing ring; no portal silhouette; no circular rim; no lens flare; no
exposure blast. Both the luminance cap and the no-ring check go into
`tools/quality.mjs` as a scripted scroll test.
Proof: five-frame transition strip. Inspection: **B**.

### V3 — INTERIOR REFERENCE IMAGES
Deliverable is **actual images**, produced in Jacob's image tool from prompts
written here. **At most three candidates, one full-frame image per file** — no
grid, no collage, no contact sheet, no embedded text, same aspect ratio. Three
distinct interior spatial grammars under the same locked story. Jacob picks one;
that file becomes implementation truth.
Nothing in I3 begins before a pick.

### I3 — THE INTERIOR WORLD
Built to match the picked image. The second awe beat: the inside is a world
impossibly larger than the outside.
**Requires:** one vast continuous spatial field; a few immense level changes,
never repeated steps; large readable light and dark territories; no human-scale
architecture anywhere; enough mid-value structure for a shadow to visibly cross;
sparse entry lights arranged by real record state.
**Forbidden:** terraces, a flat black plane with glitter, a city, a canyon, a
data visualisation, particle weather, modular sci-fi platforms, generic terrain.
The exterior podium grammar must not be inherited — it is dead.
**Mote law:** do not spawn one particle per ledger entry indefinitely.
Recent events stay individual; older entries aggregate into restrained strata
and clusters, or the world densifies into noise over time.
Proof: reveal still + wide landscape still. Inspection: A.

### I4 — THE SHADOW ROAD
The tunnel with no tunnel: a band of darkness lying across the lit interior,
travelled from inside, ending at what casts it. A tunnel is a route; a shadow
proves something exists at its source.
**Requires:** enters from off-frame, never a centred lane from the start; broad
off-axis umbra with a softer penumbra; lit terrain visible on both flanks; motes
extinguishing inside the shadow volume; distant surfaces crossing in and out of
it; sharpening as the caster nears.
**World space, not screen space** — it must affect terrain, haze, entry lights
and nearby structures. A vignette or overlay is a failure.
**Forbidden:** tunnel meshes, tubes, rings, lens-only fakery, a black road
painted down the centre.
Proof: before / entering / inside sequence (motion, not one still).
Inspection: **B**.

### I5a / I5b — THE STATIONS
Four stations along the road: `01 THE SYSTEM`, `02 DESK42`, `03 BRAWLER`,
`04 THE STUDIO`. Contact lives at the return and stays one click away.

**Each station carries only four things:** one large title; one concise
sentence; one real state/evidence detail (live authoritative counts, never
copywriting); one quiet mono world annotation.

**No persistent ledger column through the journey.** The full ledger opens only
at technology/evidence, the true-form encounter, and the return audit.

**Annotation register decays with depth** — architecture (`THE COURSES`) to
anatomy (`THE SPINE`) to restraint (`TIE 14 — LOAD`). That semantic decay is
where the discrepancy lands: the labels stop describing a building and start
describing a hold.

Top nav stays available; the right-half panel is a practical access layer, not
the cinematic presentation. Deep links land on the panel.
**I5a builds ONE station completely** (desktop + mobile) for Jacob's verdict
before I5b builds the rest. Inspection: A.

### V6 — TRUE-FORM REFERENCES
**At most three candidates, one full-frame image per file.** Same camera, same

### I6 — THE TRUE FORM
Mechanics may be prototyped **only against invisible bounding volumes, debug

Mechanics: total stillness; the one slow strain (millimetre flex of the ties,

**Acceptance:** no copy in the frame; no eye construction (never a lit void

Inspection: A.

**Three precise retained differences instead:**

## 7. Global execution rules

**Environment.** Work in this checkout on `claude/genesis`, from the HEAD
carrying the six sinister gates. Dev server: launch entry `genesis-node` (the
npm bin is broken here). Typecheck:
`node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json`.

**Token discipline is part of correctness.** Under rule A, never read a captured
PNG back into context — capture, state the path, stop. No tuning loops, no
re-capture cycles, no extra verification passes. `tools/quality.mjs` runs once at
the end of a gate. `src/render/JourneyRenderer.ts` is thousands of lines: grep
for the anchor comment, read only the surrounding range.

**Determinism.** No `Math.random` in world logic. New seeded streams use
`mulberry32(seed ^ <new salt>)`. Never reorder an existing stream — it changes
the whole world.

**Capture discipline.** Shipping page, never `?bare=1` (DOM scrims change the
frame). Pointer untouched, so the authored pose and the pre-pointer watcher
state are what is photographed. Jacob's viewport is 2270x1278.

**Traps already paid for — do not rediscover:**
- Canvas pixels are only true inside `requestAnimationFrame`.
- Playwright `evaluate` takes **one** argument; a stringified function silently
  returns undefined.
- The bloom threshold (0.78) decides visibility: a front below it is real in a
  difference image and invisible on the monitor.
- Anything standing on the plain stays under distance 2400 or it hovers.
- Objects and ground must fog toward the same colour at the same distance.
- Mean-absolute-frame-diff is blind to low-contrast motion. Look at two frames.
- Headless/SwiftShader is not GPU truth.
- Every tuned value belongs to a camera pose. The landing camera does not move.

**Untouchable during interior work:** the six sinister gates' mechanics, the
landing camera, typography, and the DOM content contract.
