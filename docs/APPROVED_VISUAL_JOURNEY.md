## What this opens

`src/`, `public/models/` and `tools/blender/` are open for THE GRAIN and for
nothing else. Any other visual direction is still closed.

Technology was chosen after the frames and not before: authored geometry from
Blender, rendered live in Three.js, in two detail tiers. The plain's masses are
generated by running one seeded generator many times, so "same rule, different
outcome" is literally true of the assets rather than illustrated by them.

## What is not approved

Motion. Nothing in this file authorises a camera path, a transition, timing or
sound. Those are studied from the approved stills, and after the F3 surface test
passes.
