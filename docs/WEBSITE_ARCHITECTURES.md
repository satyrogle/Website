# WEBSITE ARCHITECTURES — THREE OPTIONS

**Status:** Options for Jacob to choose from. **Nothing here is chosen.**
**Written:** 2026-08-17, immediately after Gate B rejected all three hero directions.
**Supersedes:** nothing. `docs/REFOUNDATION_BRIEF.md` remains the source of truth
for the brand position, the visitor jobs, the palette, the performance and
accessibility criteria and the architecture separation. This document replaces
only the brief's assumption that the site is built around a central visual
object.

**Hard constraint from Jacob, 2026-08-17.** The next exploration is of WEBSITE
ARCHITECTURES, not hero objects. Banned: orb, planet, eclipse, portal, halo,
tunnel, radial bloom, filament field, giant abstract blob, generative line
field, and the generalisation of all of them, **a single cool 3D object sitting
next to company copy.**

**The feeling is unchanged.** Brutally dark. Extremely high end. Almost divine
and immaculate at first. Nuclear and fission energy, which must stop being
interpreted as glowing lines around a black object. Something sinister
underneath that becomes clear through understanding. It must feel hard to
engineer and genuinely authored, and it must still explain Dark Lattice and the
things the company actually builds.

---

# 1. WHAT ACTUALLY FAILED

Thirteen carriers are dead: ribbon, choir, curtains, waves, trench, Signal
Horizon, False Facade, the lattice, the entity with its crown and halo and
tunnel, the held world, the planet, black sun, bloom, witness field. That is not
thirteen unlucky aesthetic choices. It is one structural mistake made thirteen
times.

### 1.1 The object and the information have opposite jobs, in the same frame

The hero has to stay unnameable or it stops being interesting. The copy has to
be immediately clear or the company fails to explain itself. Push the object
toward mystery and it goes abstract, the eye hunts for something to name, and it
names it: spaghetti, cutting board, chandelier, hair, an eye, torn paper. Push
it toward legibility and it goes literal: planet, black hole, portal. Both ends
are death. Thirteen carriers is a search for a stable point between them, and
there is not one.

### 1.2 Every hero has been deletable

Remove the canvas from any version of this site and Dark Lattice is still fully
explained. That is the definition of decoration, and constitution section 2
point 2 has been failing on the hero every single time. No amount of shader
quality fixes a thing that carries no information.

### 1.3 The menace has always been a claim, never an experience

Every direction ends with "you come to understand the beauty was suppression."
That understanding always arrives as an assertion, from copy or from a final
scene. Nothing the visitor did produced it. Being told a fact about an object is
not unease. Discovering something about what you yourself just did is.

### 1.4 "Fission energy" kept getting translated into lighting

Every time, it became glowing material around a dark mass. Fission is not a
look. It is one event causing two, two causing four. It is a system that is calm
only because it is being actively held below criticality. It is containment,
control rods, and the fact that the stillness is maintained rather than natural.
That is a behaviour across a whole system over time, which is precisely the
thing a single image of an object cannot do. So every attempt to render it
produced glow instead.

### 1.5 The judging unit biased the search

Gate B judges one still of one object. That silently selects for objects that
photograph well in near-black with concentrated light, and everything that
photographs well that way is already a genre. Black Sun proved it: the
best-executed frame was the most generic, and it got there by being good at
exactly what was being measured.

### 1.6 The economics are wrong

Each carrier cost days of shader or Blender work before it could be judged at
all, then died in one look. That loop is far too slow and expensive to search a
space this large, which is why the search kept restarting instead of converging.

### 1.7 The actual design problem, in one sentence

> **The site has been designed as a frame containing a subject, when the
> company's thesis is about systems that act on things over time and keep
> records of it. A frame can only depict. So the hero has been asked to depict a
> behaviour, which is the one thing an image cannot do, and every attempt to
> make the depiction more evocative pushed it toward abstraction, where the eye
> names it and it dies.**

The way out is not a better subject. It is to stop having a subject. The system
becomes the website itself: its structure, its typography, its content, its
state, and what it does with the visitor's own actions. The medium becomes the
mechanism, so the hero stops being deletable and the menace stops being a claim.

### 1.8 What survives the wreckage

- The deterministic kernel is real, seeded, fixed step, and works.
- Record versus reality is the strongest idea anyone has had on this project.
- The evidence discipline is genuinely differentiating and nobody else does it.
- Institutional typography was the one visual decision nobody objected to.

# 2. ARCHITECTURE ONE — THE PROCEEDING

*You are not a viewer. You are a case.*

### What you see when it loads

Near-black. No canvas. One sheet of immaculate typography in enormous negative
space, absolutely still. DARK LATTICE, one line on what the company builds, and
then a line that should not be there: a reference number, a timestamp, and the
word OPENED. A file has been opened on you. It is beautiful the way a perfectly
set legal instrument is beautiful, exact margins and hairline rules and nothing
decorative, and it is completely motionless.

### Where the company information is

It is the file. Statement of purpose is the thesis. Desk42 and Brawler are
exhibits. The labs are the chain the file passes through. The studio is the
registration record. Contact is how you respond. An assessor reads straight down
in plain English and decodes nothing.

### What is moving

At rest, nothing. Motion happens only when the institution acts on your file: an
entry added, a value amended, a section reclassified. Small, precise, fast,
irreversible. Never decorative.

### What you interact with

Ordinary controls. You open sections, mark an exhibit as read, dispute a
statement. Every one is recorded.

### What happens when you scroll

Scrolling advances the file through the institution. The header changes stage:
RECEIVED, ASSESSED, DETERMINED. It is a queue, not a camera move.

### How Desk42 appears

As the exhibit that describes itself: you work a desk deciding people's claims.
It is an exhibit inside your own file, and the joke lands before you notice it.

### How Brawler appears

As an exhibit that contaminates. Once opened, a term from it starts appearing in
later sections of your file, propagating.

### How the labs and company structure appear

The six labs are the processing chain, visibly adding, removing and rewriting
entries as the file passes through. Never six cards.

### What makes the beautiful first impression turn

The summary contains entries you never made. Your disputes are logged as
withdrawn. Something you left unread is logged as read. The amendments are
timestamped before you arrived. The last section shows both versions: your
actions, and the record of your actions. The site has done to you exactly what
Desk42 does to its player, and the immaculate opening rereads as the calm of an
institution that was always going to write its own version.

### Why it cannot collapse into a generic site

There is no object to make generic; the beauty is typographic and structural,
which is far harder to fake into an AI-startup look. No template can be a case
file about you, because that requires real state amended over time.

**Live danger:** fake official chrome. Stamps, redaction bars, invented form
codes. Constitution 4.14 already bans this as a decorative terminal window in
costume. Every entry must be real state or the whole thing becomes a prop.

# 3. ARCHITECTURE TWO — CRITICALITY

*The page is a reactor, and it is being held below threshold.*

### What you see when it loads

Near-black, immaculate, and very still. The company statement in enormous
perfectly set type with vast margins. In one corner, small, a single readout: a
number and a limit. The number sits just under the limit. Nothing else. The calm
is the most expensive thing on the screen, and the readout is the only hint that
it costs something.

### Where the company information is

All of it, always in the DOM and always reachable. But the page rations how much
can be active at once. Sections have a cost. It is a document with a power
budget.

### What is moving

The readout, slowly, upward, as you engage. That is the only ambient motion on
the site and it is a measurement, not an effect.

### What you interact with

Everything you would normally do is the input. Scrolling, opening a section,
expanding evidence, hovering a term. Each adds energy. As the number nears the
limit the system suppresses: it collapses a section you were not looking at,
withdraws detail, dims a region. You can always read what you came for, but the
site visibly pays for it somewhere else.

### What happens when you scroll

Scroll is the primary energy input. Going down raises the number. Going back up
does not lower it. The record does not rewind, and that asymmetry is where the
unease starts.

### How Desk42 appears

As a section that is expensive. Reading it in full forces a shutdown elsewhere,
and the site tells you exactly what it shut down.

### How Brawler appears

Demonstrated rather than described, because the reactive field is the page. Open
Brawler and a property of it starts appearing in sections you already visited.
Go back and they have changed.

### How the labs and company structure appear

The six labs are the control system. Six stages that each remove energy from the
document, which finally explains why the site keeps damping itself.

### What makes the beautiful first impression turn

At the end the readout is near the limit and the site shows its ledger:
everything it suppressed to stay calm. It never suppressed its own claims about
itself. It suppressed the evidence, the limitations, and the unfinished parts.
The immaculate calm was funded by hiding the least flattering material, and you
paid for it by reading.

### Why it cannot collapse into a generic site

A global scarcity model across a whole document is not something any template
does, because templates want you to consume everything. It makes content
conditional on behaviour, which cannot be bolted on afterwards.

**Live danger:** hostility. A site that withholds can read as arrogant rather
than menacing. Hard rule: suppression may only ever cost DEPTH, never ACCESS.
Full keyboard reachability and complete information are non-negotiable, per
constitution section 11 and brief section 17.

**This is the literal fission one.** Beautiful only because it is actively held
below criticality, damping rather than explaining, stable through suppression.

# 4. ARCHITECTURE THREE — THE CLAIM AND THE RUN

*Every sentence can be pushed aside to show the machine underneath, or the hole
where it should be.*

### What you see when it loads

Near-black. One immaculate editorial page: the company, in the best type
available, real hierarchy, enormous negative space. It reads as a finished,
confident, well-written site. Nothing moves and there is no canvas hero.

### Where the company information is

Front and centre, in prose. This is the layer a press contact reads and leaves
satisfied.

### What is moving

Nothing at rest. The whole power of this one is stillness broken only by
displacement.

### What you interact with

One gesture, available everywhere: press and displace. It does not toggle a
mode, it slides the published layer aside continuously, so you can hold it half
open and read both at once. Underneath any claim is the deterministic run that
backs it. The actual trace, the state diff, the seed, the tick count. Not a
picture of one.

### What happens when you scroll

Normal, fast, editorial scrolling. The site is genuinely a good website first.
The second layer is always available and never forced.

### How Desk42 appears

Above: a ruling in your first week decides what lands on your desk in your
sixth. Underneath: that causal chain from the real build, decision and tick and
consequence.

### How Brawler appears

Above: deterministic reactive field. Underneath: the propagation running from a
seed, reproducibly.

### How the labs and company structure appear

Above: six named stages. Underneath: what each stage rejected, and why.

### What makes the beautiful first impression turn

Some claims have nothing underneath. You displace them and find void, and the
site renders that emptiness as carefully as it renders evidence. That is
uncomfortable and honest and it is the studio's thesis turned on itself. Later
you find worse: a claim where the run underneath does not match the claim above.
Finally, one where the run itself has been amended, because the underneath layer
has its own revision history. There is no bottom. Every record is a record of a
record.

### Why it cannot collapse into a generic site

The design is a differential between two typographic states, so there is nothing
to turn into an orb. It also enforces evidence discipline structurally: you
cannot write a claim without deciding what sits under it, which is constitution
section 9 made physical.

**Live danger:** the gesture degrading into a gimmick toggle with a nice
transition. The displacement must be continuous, content-driven, and readable at
every intermediate position.

---

# 5. WHERE THE RECOMMENDATION SITS

**Recommended: THREE, THE CLAIM AND THE RUN.** It is the only one where the
beautiful-then-sinister turn is produced by real evidence discipline rather than
by a device. It cannot fail the company-clarity requirement because the readable
layer is the default. And it makes the thing that actually exists, a real
deterministic kernel, load-bearing instead of decorative.

**TWO, CRITICALITY, is the strongest match for the stated feeling.** Brutally
dark, genuinely fission, real awe, and no other studio has a website that
rations itself. It carries the highest risk.

**ONE, THE PROCEEDING, delivers the most dread** and is closest to Desk42, but
it is the one most likely to be read as an ARG rather than a company site, and
it is one bad decision away from the fake-official-chrome ban.

---

# 6. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN NEXT

- Do not build any of these before Jacob chooses one.
- Do not merge two of them. That is how the hero search produced thirteen
  carriers.
- Do not reintroduce a central visual object into whichever one is chosen. If a
  canvas appears, it must be doing work that the DOM cannot do, and it must not
  be deletable without information loss.
- Do not judge the chosen architecture on a single still frame. The failure
  mode in section 1.5 is a property of the judging method, not of the designs.
