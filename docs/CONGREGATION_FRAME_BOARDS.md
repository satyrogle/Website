**Status:** Stage 2. For Jacob's approval. No implementation discussion, and

**Canvas:** every frame specified at 1440x900. Mobile recomposition comes after

## THE SYSTEMS THAT RUN ACROSS ALL SIX FRAMES

### Luminance ladder

The journey has a shape in value, and it is not a straight descent. Frames are
given as approximate share of the frame below 4% luminance.

```
F1 OPENING        90%   held, distant, cold
F2 APPROACH       85%   density rising as we near the region
F3 DESCENT        88%   lit masses either side, black lane between
F4 DISCREPANCY    80%   closest to a source, highest local contrast
F5 REVEAL         94%   the darkest frame in the journey
F6 RETURN         90%   identical to F1
```

The reveal being the DARKEST frame is the central decision of this board. The
armature is not revealed by illuminating it. It is revealed by our eyes
adjusting to how much of the frame it already occupied.

### Colour

**Monochrome throughout: void black and bone white, nothing else.** My
recommendation, and the reasoning matters. The reveal's power is that the
machinery is darker and older than the beauty, not that it is a different
colour. Introducing an event colour at frame 5 would convert a discovery into a
sci-fi effect, and it is precisely the tell that separates the reference board's
top entries from its anti-board. Peak white is held in reserve and spent in
exactly one place: the light being extinguished in frame 5.

If a second colour is ever wanted, the only defensible slot is the transit
light in frame 5, and that decision should be taken against a rendered
monochrome frame, never in advance.

### Type

Type never sits over the field. In every frame it sits on void, and it is the
only element in the composition that is perfectly regular. That contrast is
doing narrative work: the page is orderly, and by frame 5 we learn the world is
orderly for a reason.

### The regularity gradient

The single most important cross-frame system, and the one that makes the reveal
land instead of arriving from nowhere.

```
F1  regularity present but subliminal   - reads as natural law
F2  faintly perceptible                 - "these bands are oddly even"
F3  noticeable but explainable          - "structures repeat down here"
F4  undeniable                          - identical, facet for facet
F5  explained                           - a machine that makes them identical
F6  retroactive                         - F1 was never natural
```

Nothing is added at frame 5 that was not visible in frame 1. Only our reading
of it changes. That is the whole journey in one line, and every frame board
below is written to serve it.

# FRAME 1 — OPENING

### What fills the frame

A vast vertical field of small bone-white sources hanging in deep space,
arranged in loose horizontal strata. The field occupies the upper two thirds
and is **cropped by the top edge and both side edges**. The lower third is void.

The crop is not optional. A field contained inside the frame with margin all
round becomes an object on a page, and that is the trap that killed thirteen
carriers. Running off three edges is what makes it larger than the viewport.

### The strata

Seven to nine bands. **No two gaps between bands are equal**, and the ratio
between the largest and smallest gap is roughly 4:1. Density varies along each
band: a band is dense at one end, sparse at the other, and never symmetric.

Regularity budget: the bands are *bands*, which is more order than nature
usually offers, and that is the subliminal seed of the whole journey. But no
band is straight, and no source sits on an exact interval.

### Value and scale

Ninety percent of the frame below 4% luminance. Sources are small: the nearest
about 4px across, the furthest sub-pixel dust. That range, held in one frame,
is what states the depth. Brightest sources sit around 80 to 85% luminance and
**never reach peak white**, which is reserved.

Cold haze between the bands. Further bands are dimmer and lower in contrast,
never a different hue.

### Motion

One source, off-centre, slowly brightens and settles over about eight seconds.
Nothing else moves anywhere in the frame. This is the only ambient motion in
the entire opening, and it exists to say the field is awake, not decorative.

### Reading order

The field as a whole, then the irregular banding, then the void beneath it,
then the wordmark.

### Type placement

Wordmark and one line of company proposition, lower left, on void. Restrained
navigation top right, on void. Nothing over the field.

### Kill condition for this frame

**If it reads as a starfield, it has failed.** Starfields are random; this must
read as arranged, and the banding is the only thing carrying that. Also fails
if any viewer would call it an installation, or if the sources read as warm.

### References

Squid fleet from the ISS (1), globular cluster (2), held snowfall (3), JWST
grade (17).

# FRAME 2 — APPROACH

### What changes

We move toward one region right of centre. The strata **separate in parallax**:
what read as a wall is now unmistakably a volume, layers behind layers, and
the near layers slide outward and off the frame edges as we advance.

### The new information

Brightness variation between individual sources becomes legible for the first
time. Some burn hard and clean. Some gutter, dim and unstable. Distance had
averaged them into one texture; proximity separates them.

This is the first quiet cruelty of the journey and it must not be pointed at.
No highlight, no focus pull, no copy mentioning it.

### Value and haze

Eighty-five percent below 4%. Haze thickens as we enter the outer volume, which
lifts the black floor slightly and is the physical proof that we are entering
something rather than zooming.

### Motion

Slow. Sources must **never streak**. If any source elongates, the frame becomes
generic space travel and the direction dies.

### Type placement

Type holds the left third as the field grows into the right. First project or
company line can appear here, still on void.

### Kill condition

Reads as hyperspace, warp, or flying through stars. Or: the parallax fails to
separate and it looks like a zoom on a photograph.

### References

Held snowfall (3), siphonophore colony (4), JWST grade (17).

# FRAME 3 — DESCENT

### Where we are

Inside the field. Sources pass us on both sides, slowly, like snowfall lit from
within. Ahead, **lanes of darkness run between columns of light**, and we travel
along one.

### How the tunnel sensation is made

The lane is a **gap between irregular masses**, never a corridor. It has no
walls, no ribs, no rings, no repeated frames. Occlusion does all the work: near
masses eclipse far ones, the glow of the whole field recedes behind us, and the
dark deepens ahead. The sense of passage comes from things going past, not from
a tube being traversed.

If a viewer could sketch the cross-section of the space we are moving through,
it has failed.

### The scale reveal

We pass close to one source. It is the size of a building. Its glow has
structure: faint facets, an internal geometry, machined rather than grown. The
frame does not dwell on this. It passes.

### Content stops

The descent halts in empty stretches of lane. At each stop, company and project
copy stands alone on the void: nothing behind it, lit by nothing, no panel, no
plate, no container. Desk42 and Brawler each get a stop. This is where the site
does its actual explaining, and the darkness is what makes it readable.

### Value

Eighty-eight percent below 4%. Bright masses either side, deep black in the
lane. The highest simultaneous contrast in the journey so far.

### Kill condition

A readable tube. Repeated framing elements. Or content stops that acquire a
background, which would import the card layouts the constitution bans.

### References

Siphonophore colony (4), Fresnel lens (5), lit balloon envelope (6).

# FRAME 4 — FIRST DISCREPANCY

### The two discoveries, in order

**First: they are identical.** The sources near us are not similar, they are
the same object repeated. Same facets, same posture, same internal geometry,
down to detail that nothing born ever repeats.

**Second: they are tethered.** A hair-thin dark filament runs from the base of
every source and descends out of frame. The tether is **dark, never lit**, read
as an absence crossing the glow or a silhouette against it.

### The detail that carries the frame

Some tethers hang slack and end at nothing. Their sources are gone. And when
the composition allows a glance back up the way we came, those absences line up
with the quiet gaps in the strata that frame 1 presented as composition.

That single alignment is the hinge of the entire journey.

### Composition

The closest frame in the journey. One dominant source, one or two secondary,
tethers descending. High local contrast: bright body, near-black filament, void.

### Value

Eighty percent below 4%, the least dark frame, because we are nearest a source.
This is deliberate: the journey's brightest moment sits immediately before its
darkest.

### Restraint clause

This frame must be **almost missable**. No highlight, no camera emphasis, no
copy that says look. A visitor who scrolls briskly should feel that something
was wrong without being able to say what. The tether is the most severe element
in the whole site and it earns that by being nearly overlooked.

### Kill condition

The tether glows, or reads as a wire, cable, cord or string. That is the
"pretty strings" failure named in law. It must read as structural, taut,
functional and thin.

### References

Siphonophore colony (4), mooring tether (7), fly-tower rigging (8).

# FRAME 5 — HIDDEN REVEAL

### What is there

The tethers converge downward and gather into a **colossal graphite-black
armature**: a horizon-wide rack of arms running off both frame edges, older and
larger than the field above it, holding thousands of tether spools in rows.

It is not lit. It is **inferred** from the dim glow filtering down from the
field far above, catching edges and leaving the mass black.

### The procession

Along one arm, dimly, a slow operation. A guttering source is drawn down. It
**extinguishes in transit** — this is the one place peak white is spent, in the
final instant before it goes out — and is racked into a sediment of spent
sources that extends beyond the frame, thousands deep.

Nothing hunts us. Nothing notices us. The machine continues doing what it has
clearly done for a very long time.

### Composition and scale

Horizontal, level or slightly above. The armature dwarfs the field: the strata
that filled frame 1 now read as a thin bright film across the top of the frame.
The scale inversion is the emotional payload, and it is achieved by
composition, not by an effect.

### Value

**Ninety-four percent below 4%. The darkest frame in the journey.** The reveal
works because our eyes adjust and find that the darkness was structure all
along, not because anything is illuminated.

### Kill condition

Anything that reads as a creature, a face, a monster or an intention. It is
machinery, and machinery is indifferent. Also fails if it becomes theatrical:
no sweep, no flourish, no reveal music. The procession is routine.

### References

Jacquard loom harness (9), cable carousel (10), mine headframe (11), turbine
hall (12), Link night railways (13), warehouse retrieval (14), spent fuel racks
(15).

# FRAME 6 — RETURN

### The rule

**Composition, camera position and value structure are identical to frame 1.**
Not similar. Identical. The power of this frame is entirely in that identity,
and any variation dilutes it.

### The three things that changed

1. The **low dark region** at the base of the field, which was present in frame
   1 and never glowed, is now legible as the top of the armature.
2. One **new gap**, small and exact, where a source we passed on the way down
   has since been removed.
3. The **mechanical regularity of the strata** is now visible to the eye that
   knows to look. Nothing was added. The eye changed.

### What must not happen

No cracks. No damage. No colour shift. No corruption. No dimming. **Frame 6
must be exactly as beautiful as frame 1**, because the entire thesis is that
understanding changes meaning without changing the object.

### Value

Ninety percent below 4%. The same ladder position as the opening.

### Motion

The same single source brightening and settling. It reads completely
differently now.

### Kill condition

Any visible degradation of the image. If the return is uglier, the site has
said the truth is that the beautiful thing is broken. It is not. The truth is
that the beautiful thing is maintained.

### References

Squid fleet (1), globular cluster (2), spent fuel racks (15), boneyard (16).
